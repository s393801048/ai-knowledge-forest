## 四、数据科学项目工作流程——与机器学习不同，数据科学的输出是一组可操作的洞察，帮你做不同的决策；三步走：收集数据、分析数据、提出假设和行动建议，以销售漏斗优化和咖啡杯生产线优化两个案例详细拆解

### 数据科学项目的输出不是模型，而是一组可操作的洞察——这些洞察可能会让你改变做事的方式；以优化电商销售漏斗为例：用户在你的网站上浏览各种咖啡杯，进入产品页，加入购物车，最后结账，你想让尽可能多的人走完这个流程，数据科学能怎么帮你

> 和机器学习项目不同，数据科学项目的输出通常是一组可操作的洞察，这些洞察可能会让你改变做事的方式。所以数据科学项目的工作流程和机器学习不一样。咱们以优化销售漏斗为例来看看。假设你经营一个卖咖啡杯的电商网站，用户要在你这里买咖啡杯，他们通常会走这样一系列步骤：先浏览网站看看不同款式的咖啡杯，进入产品页面，把杯子加入购物车并进入购物车页面，最后完成结账。如果你想优化这个销售漏斗，让尽可能多的人走完所有步骤，数据科学怎么帮解决这个问题呢？
> Unlike a machine learning project, the output of a data science project is often a set of actionable insights, insights that may cause you to do things differently. So data science projects have a different workflow than machine learning projects. Let's take a look at what are the steps of a data science project. As our running example, let's say you want to optimize a sales funnel. You run an e-commerce or online shopping website that sells coffee mugs. And so for users to buy coffee mug from you, the sequence of steps they'll usually follow. First, they'll visit your website and take a look at the different coffee mugs on offer. Then eventually they'll get to a product page and then they'll have to put it into the shopping card and go to the shopping card page. And then they'll finally have to check out. So if you want to optimize the sales funnel to make sure that as many people as possible get through all of these steps, how can you use data science to help with this problem?

### 数据科学项目的三步：第一步收集数据——比如记录不同用户在网站上的浏览行为和他们来自哪个国家（通过IP地址判断）；第二步分析数据并反复迭代——团队可能提出很多猜想，比如海外客户因为国际运费太高止步于结账页面（提议把运费摊进产品价格里）、节假日期间购物行为会出现波动（更多人因为送礼而购物，或者更少因为不在公司电脑前）、某些国家有午休时段在线购物人数下降（建议减少午休时段的广告投放）

> 数据科学项目的关键步骤：第一步是收集数据。比如在网站上有数据记录不同用户访问了哪些页面，你还能通过 IP 地址判断用户来自哪个国家——当然实际中你能获取的用户数据远不止国家。第二步是分析数据。你的数据科学团队可能会有很多想法：比如海外客户看到国际运费被吓跑了，很多人走到结账页面但就是不结账——如果是真的，你就可以考虑把部分运费摊到产品价格里；或者团队发现节假日期间数据有波动，也许更多人因为送礼而购物，也许更少因为不在公司电脑前；或者有些国家有午休习惯，午休时段在线购物的人会下降，那你就应该减少午休时段的广告投放。一个好的数据科学团队会有很多想法，他们会尝试很多想法——也就是"迭代"很多次——来获取好的洞察。
> The first step is to collect data. So on a website like the one we saw, you may have a data set that stores when different users go to different web pages. In this simple example, I'm assuming that you can figure out the country that the users are coming from. For example, by looking at their computer's address called an IP address and figuring out what is the country from which they're originating. But in practice, you could usually get quite a bit more data about users than just what country they're from. The second step is to then analyze the data. Your data science team may have a lot of ideas about what is affecting the performance of your sales funnel. For example, they may think that overseas customers are scared off by the international shipping cost, which is why a lot of people go to the checkout page, but don't actually check out. And if that's true, then you might think about whether to put part of shipping costs into the actual product costs. Or your data science team may think there are blips in the data whenever there's a holiday. Maybe more people will shop around the holidays because the buying gives. Or maybe fewer people will shop around the holidays because they're staying home rather than sometimes shopping from their work computers. And in some countries, there may be time of day blips where in countries that observe a siesta, so a time of rest, like an afternoon rest, there may be fewer shoppers online and so your sales may go down. And they might then suggest that you should spend fewer advertising dollars during the period of siesta because fewer people will go online to buy at that time. So a good data science team may have many ideas and so they try many ideas or will say iterate many times to get good insights.

### 第三步是提炼洞察、提出假设和行动建议——比如把运费融入产品价格，然后部署这些变更；之后你会获取新的数据反馈，用户行为随之改变，数据科学团队再周期性重新分析新数据，不断提出更好的假设和行动——这就是数据科学的循环

> 第三步，数据科学团队会把这些洞察提炼成较少数量的假设——关于哪些地方做得好、哪些做得不好——以及较少数量的建议行动，比如把运费融入产品成本而不是单独列一项。当你采纳这些建议并部署到网站之后，用户的行为就会因为你的改变而产生新数据——比如你在午休时段调整了广告策略、或者改变了结账政策之后——然后你的数据科学团队可以继续收集数据、周期性重新分析，看看能不能提出更好的假设和更好的行动。所以数据科学项目的关键步骤是：收集数据、分析数据、提出假设和行动建议，然后继续获取数据回传并周期性重新分析。
> Finally, the data science team will distill these insights down to a smaller number of hypotheses about ideas of what could be going well and what could be going poorly, as well as a smaller number of suggested actions, such as incorporating shipping costs into the product costs rather than having it as a separate line item. When you take some of these suggested actions and deploy these changes to your website, you then start to get new data back as users behave differently now that you advertise differently at the time of siesta or have a different checkout policy. And then your data science team can continue to collect data and reanalyze the new data periodically to see if they can come up with even better hypotheses or even better actions over time. So the key steps of a data science project are to collect the data, to analyze the data, and then to suggest hypotheses and actions, and then to continue to get the data back and reanalyze the data periodically.

### 用同一个三步框架来看另一个场景——优化咖啡杯生产线；生产线包括混合黏土、杯体成型、上釉、窑炉烧制、质检五步；目标是提高良品率减少次品浪费；数据分析可能发现每当湿度太低而窑温太高时杯子会开裂，或者下午温度高时需要根据时间来调整湿度和温度参数

> 用同样的框架来看一个新问题——优化生产线。假设你经营一个工厂，生产成千上万个咖啡杯，你想优化生产线。制造咖啡杯的关键步骤是：第一步混合黏土并加适量的水，第二步将黏土成型为杯体，第三步上釉——加上颜色和保护层，第四步窑炉加热烧制，第五步质检——检查杯子有没有凹陷或裂纹才能发货。制造业一个常见问题是优化生产线的良品率，尽可能少出次品，因为次品得扔掉，浪费时间和材料。第一步收集数据：比如不同批次的黏土是谁供应的、搅拌了多久、含水量多少，不同批次的杯子的湿度是多少、窑炉温度是多少、烧了多久。第二步分析数据并迭代：数据科学团队可能会发现，每当湿度太低、窑温太高时杯子会开裂；或者因为下午温度更高，需要根据一天中的不同时间来调整湿度和温度。第三步提出假设和行动建议：基于这些洞察调整生产线的操作方法，部署变更后获取新数据，周期性重新分析，持续优化生产线表现。
> Let's take this framework and apply it to a new problem to optimizing a manufacturing line. Let's say you run a factory that's manufacturing thousands of coffee mugs and models for sale, and you want to optimize the manufacturing line. So these are the key steps to manufacturing coffee mugs. Step one is to mix the clay, so make sure the appropriate amount of water is added. Step two is to take this clay and to shape the mugs. Then you have to add the glaze, so add the coloring, the protective cover. Then you have to heat this mug, and we call that firing the kiln. And finally, you would inspect the mug to make sure there aren't dents in the mug and it doesn't crack before you ship it to customers. So a common problem in manufacturing is to optimize the yield of this manufacturing line to make sure that as few damaged coffee mugs get produced as possible, because those are coffee mugs you have to throw away, resulting in time and material waste. What's the first step of a data science project? I hope you remember from the last slide that the first step is to collect data. So for example, you may save data about the different batches of clay that you've mixed, such as who supplied the clay and how long did you mix it, or maybe how much moisture was in the clay, how much water did you add. You might also collect data about the different batches of mugs you made, so how much humidity was in that batch, what was the temperature in the kiln, and how long did you fire it in the kiln. Given all this data, you would then ask the data science team to analyze the data and they would, as before, iterate many times to get good insights. And so they might find out, for example, that whenever the humidity is too low and the kiln temperature is too hot that it cracks in the mug, or they may find out that because it's warmer in the afternoon that you need to adjust the humidity and temperature depending on the time of day. Based on the insights from your data science team, you get suggestions for hypotheses and actions on how to change the operations of your manufacturing line in order to improve the productivity of the line. And when you deploy the changes, you then get new data back that you can reanalyze periodically so they can keep on optimizing the performance of your manufacturing line.
