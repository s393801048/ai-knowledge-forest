## 八、深度学习解释（第1部分）——神经网络就像搭乐高积木，每个神经元计算一个简单函数，堆叠起来就能算出极其复杂的A到B映射，而且中间那些层全是你不需要操心的——AI自己搞定

### 用T恤需求预测来直观理解——假设你运营一个卖T恤的网站，价格越高需求越低，你可以拟合一条曲线：价格作为输入A，预估需求作为输出B；这个蓝色曲线就是最简单的神经网络，中间那个小圆圈就叫"神经元"（人工神经元），它做的事就是计算这条曲线

> 在AI中，"深度学习"和"神经网络"这两个词几乎可以互换使用。虽然它们做机器学习非常厉害，但围绕它们也有一些炒作和神秘感。这个视频会帮你揭开深度学习的神秘面纱，让你理解深度学习和神经网络到底是什么。咱们用一个需求预测的例子。假设你运营一个卖T恤的网站，你想知道——根据你怎么定价——你预计能卖出多少件T恤。你可能会创建这样一个数据集：T恤价格越高，需求越低。你可能会拟合一条直线穿过这些数据，显示出价格上升、需求下降。但需求不可能降到零以下，所以你可能会说需求在零这里趋于平坦——超过某个点之后，你预计基本没人会买T恤了。结果，这条蓝色线条可能就是最简单的神经网络。你把价格作为输入A，你想让它输出预估需求B。用神经网络的方式来画：价格输入到这个小小的圆形物体，然后这个小圆形物体输出预估需求。在AI的术语中，这个小圆形物体叫"神经元"——有时候叫"人工神经元"——它做的事情就是计算我左边画的这条蓝色曲线。这大概是最简单的神经网络了——只有一个单个人工神经元，输入价格，输出预估需求。如果你把这个橙色圆圈、这个人工神经元想象成一块小乐高积木——神经网络不过就是拿很多这样的乐高积木堆叠起来，堆出一个强大的、由这些神经元构成的大型网络。

> The terms deep learning and neural network are used almost interchangeably in AI. And even though they're great for machine learning, there's also been a bit of hype and a bit of mystique about them. This video will demystify deep learning so that you have a sense of what deep learning and neural networks really are. Let's use an example from demand prediction. Let's say you run a website that sells t-shirts and you want to know based on how you price the t-shirts, how many units you expect to sell, how many t-shirts you expect to sell. You might then create a data set like this where the higher the price of the t-shirt, the lower the demand. So you might fill a straight line to this data showing that as the price goes up, the demand goes down. Now demand can never go below zero, so maybe you say that the demand will flatten out at zero and beyond a certain point, you expect pretty much no one to buy any t-shirts. It turns out this blue line is maybe the simplest possible neural network. You have as input the price, A, and you want it to output the estimated demand, B. So the way you would draw this as a neural network is that the price would be input to this little round thing there and this little round thing outputs the estimated demand. In the terminology of AI, this little round thing here is called a neuron or sometimes it's called an artificial neuron and all it does is compute this blue curve that I've drawn here on the left. This is maybe the simplest possible neural network with a single artificial neuron that just inputs the price and outputs the estimated demand. If you think of this orange circle, this artificial neuron as a little Lego brick, all that the neural network is is if you take a lot of these Lego bricks and stack them on top of each other until you get a big power, a big network of these neurons.

### 再看一个更复杂的例子——除了价格，你还知道运费、市场营销预算和T恤材质（厚棉还是薄料）；你清楚顾客很在意性价比，所以一个蓝色神经元负责估算"可负担性"（主要由价格和运费决定）；另一个神经元负责估算"知名度"（主要取决于营销预算）；第三个神经元负责估算"感知质量"——这个比较微妙，既受营销影响（广告说服人这是高质量产品），也受价格影响（贵的东西有时候让人觉得质量更好）；然后这三个神经元的输出再汇入一个黄色神经元，综合算出最终的需求预测量

> 来看一个更复杂的例子。假设除了T恤的价格，你还知道顾客需要付的运费，你每周在营销上可能花多花少，你还可能用厚实昂贵的棉料或更便宜的轻质材料做T恤。这些就是你认为会影响T恤需求的因素。我们来看看一个更复杂的神经网络会是什么样子。你知道顾客非常在意"负担得起"的程度。所以我们有一个神经元——我画蓝色的这个——它的工作就是估算T恤的可负担性。可负担性主要取决于T恤的价格和运费。第二，影响T恤需求的还有"知名度"——消费者在多大程度上知道你在卖这款T恤？影响知名度的主要因素是营销。所以我在这里画第二个蓝色人工神经元——输入营销预算（你花了多少钱做营销），输出消费者对你T恤的知名度。最后，产品的"感知质量"也会影响需求。感知质量受营销影响——如果营销试图说服人们这是一件高质量T恤；有时候东西的价格也影响感知质量——贵的东西让人觉得质量更好。所以我在这里画第三个蓝色人工神经元——输入价格、营销和材质——来尝试估算T恤的感知质量。最后，既然前面这三个蓝色神经元已经分别算出了可负担性、消费者知名度和感知质量，你就可以在右边再加一个黄色神经元——接收这三个因素作为输入，输出预估需求。这就是一个神经网络——它的任务是学习从这四个输入（即输入A）到输出B（需求）的映射。也就是说它学习这个输入到输出、A到B的映射。这是一个相当小的神经网络——只有四个人工神经元。实际中，今天用的神经网络要大得多，动辄几千、几万甚至远远更多的神经元。

> Let's look at a more complex example. Suppose that instead of knowing only the price of the t-shirts, you also have the shipping cost that the customers will have to pay to get the t-shirts. Maybe you spend more or less on marketing in a given week and you can also make the t-shirt out of a thick, heavy, expensive cotton or a much cheaper, more lightweight material. These are some of the factors that you think will affect the demand for your t-shirts. Let's see what a more complex neural network might look like. You know that your customers care a lot about affordability. So let's say you have one neuron and let me draw this one in blue whose job it is to estimate the affordability of the t-shirts because affordability is mainly a function of the price of the shirts and of the shipping costs. A second thing that will affect the demand for your t-shirts is awareness. How much are consumers aware that you're selling this t-shirt? So the main thing that affects awareness is going to be your marketing. So let me draw here a second artificial neuron that inputs your marketing budget, how much you spent on marketing and outputs how aware are consumers of your t-shirt. Finally, the perceived quality of your product will also affect demand and perceived quality would be affected by marketing. If the marketing tries to convince people this is a high quality t-shirt and sometimes the price of something also affects perceived quality. So I'm going to draw here a third artificial neuron that inputs price, marketing and material and tries to estimate the perceived quality of your t-shirts. Finally, now that the earlier neurons, these three blue neurons have figured out how affordable, how much consumer awareness and what's the perceived quality, you can then have one more neural neuron over here that takes this input, these three factors and outputs the estimated demand. So this is a neural network and its job is to learn to map from these four inputs, that's the input A, to the output B to demand. So it learns this input output or A to B mapping. This is a fairly small neural network with just four artificial neurons. In practice, neural networks used today are much larger with easily thousands, tens of thousands or even much larger than that numbers of neurons.

### 但神经网络最神奇的地方在于：你根本不需要自己去找出"可负担性、知名度、感知质量"这些中间因素——你只需要把输入A（价格、运费、营销、材质）和输出B（实际需求数据）喂给它，神经网络自己就会自动学会中间那些蓝色神经元应该算什么；只要数据够多、网络够大，它就能自动找到从A到B最准确的映射函数

> 这个描述中还有一个细节我需要澄清一下：在我描述神经网络的方式中，听起来好像你得自己先搞清楚关键因素是"可负担性"、"知名度"和"感知质量"。但用神经网络最棒的一点恰恰是：要训练一个神经网络——也就是说，用神经网络来构建一个机器学习系统——你唯一需要做的就是给它输入A和输出B，它自己会搞定中间所有这些东西。所以，构建一个神经网络时，你要做的就是：喂给它大量包含输入A的数据，然后有一个像这样的神经网络——几个蓝色神经元汇聚到一个黄色输出神经元——你还需要给它包含需求B的数据。软件自己会去搞清楚这些蓝色神经元应该计算什么，从而完全自动地学习出从输入A到输出B的最准确可能的映射函数。而且事实证明，如果你给这个网络足够多的数据、训练一个足够大的神经网络，它就能非常出色地从输入A映射到输出B。所以这就是神经网络——它是一组人工神经元，每个神经元计算一个相对简单的函数，当你像搭乐高积木一样把它们堆叠到足够多，它们就能计算出极其复杂的函数，给你从输入A到输出B的非常精确的映射。

> Now there's just one final detail of this description that I want to clean up, which is that in the way I've described a neural network, it was as if you had to figure out that the key factors are affordability, awareness and perceived quality. One of the wonderful things about using neural networks is that to train a neural network, in other words, to build a machine learning system using a neural network, all you have to do is give it the input A and the output B and it figures out all of the things in the middle by itself. So to build a neural network, what you would do is feed it lots of data with the input A and have a neural network that just looks like this with a few blue neurons feeding to a yellow output neuron. And then you have to give it data with the demand B as well. And it's the software's job to figure out what these blue neurons should be computing so that it can completely automatically learn the most accurate possible function mapping from the input A to the output B. And it turns out that if you give this enough data and train a neural network that is big enough, this can do an incredibly good job mapping from inputs A to outputs B. So that's a neural network. It's a group of artificial neurons, each of which computes a relatively simple function, that when you stack enough of them together like LEGO bricks, they can compute incredibly complicated functions that give you very accurate mappings from the input A to the output B.
