## 一、AI偏见与歧视——AI从互联网文本中学习，会把人类语言中的不健康刻板印象也学进去：它认为"男人之于程序员，正如女人之于家庭主妇"；好消息是我们现在对减少AI偏见的思路比减少人类偏见更清晰

### 微软研究团队发现：让AI阅读互联网文本后，AI会学到不健康的刻板印象——当你问AI类比题："男人之于女人，正如国王之于___"，它会回答"王后"（这挺合理）；但当你问"男人之于计算机程序员，正如女人之于___"，它却回答"家庭主妇"——这个偏见答案非常遗憾；我们希望AI理解的是男女都可以平等地做程序员，也都可以平等地做家庭主妇

> AI 系统怎么会变得有偏见、从而歧视某些人？我们该怎么尝试减少甚至消除 AI 系统中的这种影响？我们从一个例子讲起。微软的一个研究团队发现了一个引人注目的结果：当 AI 从互联网上的文本学习时，它会学到不健康的刻板印象。值得称赞的是，他们也提出了减少这类 AI 系统中偏见程度的技术方案。他们发现了什么？让 AI 阅读互联网上的文本，AI 就能学到词汇，然后你可以让它做类比推理。你可以"考"这个 AI 系统。好了，你已经读完了互联网上所有文本——在"男人之于女人，正如父亲之于___"这个类比中，AI 会输出"母亲"这个词，这反映了这些词在互联网上的典型用法。如果你问"男人之于女人，正如国王之于___"，同一个 AI 系统会回答——"国王之于王后"，这在互联网用语的语境下似乎也挺合理。但研究还发现了下面这个结果：如果你问它"男人之于计算机程序员，正如女人之于___"，同一个 AI 系统给出的答案是——"女人之于家庭主妇"。我觉得这个答案真的非常遗憾。要是由我们来修正偏见，答案应该是"女人之于计算机程序员"。如果我们希望 AI 系统理解男人和女人可以平等地做计算机程序员，就像男人和女人可以平等地做家庭主妇一样，那么它应该输出"男人之于计算机程序员，正如女人之于计算机程序员"，同时也应该输出"男人之于家庭主妇，正如女人之于家庭主妇"。
> How does an AI system become biased and therefore discriminate against some people? And how can we try to reduce or eliminate this effect in our AI systems? Let's start with an example. A group at Microsoft found this remarkable result that when AI learns from text found on the internet, it can learn unhealthy stereotypes. To the credit, they also proposed technical solutions for reducing the amount of bias in this type of AI system. Here's what they found. By having an AI read text on the internet, it can learn about words and you can ask it to reason about analogies. So you can quiz the AI system. Now that you've read all this text on the internet, in the analogy man is the woman, as father is the what. So the AI will output the word mother, which reflects the way these words are typically used on the internet. If you ask it, man is the woman, as king is the what, then the same AI system will say as king is the queen, which again seems reasonable relative to the way these words are used on the internet. The research has also found the following result, which is that if you ask it, man is the computer programmer, as woman is the what, that the same AI system would output the answer woman is to homemaker. And I think this answer is really unfortunate. Let's buy as answer would be a word to say, woman is to computer programmer. If we want our AI system to understand that men and women can equally be computer programmers, just as men and women can equally be homemakers, then we would like it to output man is the computer programmer, as women is computer programmer, and also man is the homemaker, as women is the homemaker.

### AI偏见的技术原理：AI用一组数字（向量）来存储每个词，数字来自该词在互联网上的使用统计——比如"男人"表示为(1,1)，"计算机程序员"表示为(3,2)，"女人"表示为(2,3)；做类比推理时，AI会构建一个平行四边形，从"男人"到"程序员"是右移2步上移1步，从"女人"也右移2步上移1步就落在(4,4)，而"家庭主妇"恰好被放在了(4,4)的位置——这就是偏见产生的数学机制

> AI 系统怎么就从数据中学到了这样的偏见呢？我们稍微深入一点看技术细节。AI 系统存储词汇的方式是用一组数字。比如，"男人"这个词被存储——有时也说被"表示"——为两个数字 1 和 1。AI 系统怎么得到这些数字的呢？是通过统计"男人"这个词在互联网上怎么被使用。这些数字的具体计算过程相当复杂，这里就不展开了。但这些数字代表了这些词的典型用法。实践中，AI 可能用成百上千个数字来存储一个词，但这里只用两个数字来简化例子。我把这个数字画在图表上——所以"男人"我就画在右边这张图的(1,1)位置。通过观察"计算机程序员"这个词组在互联网上的使用统计，AI 会得到另一对数字，比如(3,2)，来存储或者说表示"计算机程序员"。同理，通过观察"女人"这个词怎么被使用，它会得到另一对数字，比如(2,3)，来存储或表示"女人"。当你让 AI 系统去计算上面的类比——男人之于计算机程序员，正如女人之于___——AI 系统会构建一个这样的平行四边形。它会问：哪个词关联到了(4,4)这个位置？因为 AI 认为这就是这个类比的答案。从数学角度理解：AI 认为男人和计算机程序员之间的关系是——从"男人"出发，往右走2步，往上走1步。所以它从"女人"出发，也向右2步、向上1步来找到对应词。不幸的是，当这些数字来自互联网文本时，AI 系统发现"家庭主妇"在互联网中的用法导致它被放在了(4,4)的位置——这就是 AI 系统得出这个偏见类比的数学原因。
> How does an AI system learn to become biased like this from data? Let's dive a bit more into the technical details. The way an AI system stores words is using a set of numbers. So let's say the word man is stored, or we sometimes say represented as the two numbers one one. The way an AI system comes up with these numbers is through statistics of how the word man is used on the internet. The specific process for how these numbers are computed is quite complex, and I won't go into that here. But these numbers represent the typical usage of these words. In practice, an AI might have hundreds or thousands of numbers to store a word, but I'm just going to use two numbers here to keep the examples simpler. Let me take this number and plot it on a chart. So the word man, I'm going to plot at the position one one on the figure on the right. By looking at the statistics of how the words or how the phrase computer programmer is used on the internet, the AI will have a different pair of numbers, say three two, to store or to represent the phrase computer programmer. And similarly, by looking at how the word women is used, it'll come up with a different pair of numbers, say two three, to store or to represent the word women. When you ask the AI system to compute the analogy above, man is the computer programmer as women is the what, then what the AI system will do is construct a parallelogram that looks like this. And it will ask what is the word associated with the position four four, because they will think that is the answer to this analogy. One way to think about this mathematically is that the AI thinks the relationship of man to computer programmer is that you start from the word man, go two steps to the right and one step up. And so to find the same answer for women as to what you would also go two steps to the right and one step up. Unfortunately, when these numbers are derived from text on the internet and AI system finds that the way the word homemaker is used on the internet causes it to be placed at the position four four, which is why the AI system comes up with this bias analogy.

### AI偏见在真实世界中有严重后果——某公司用AI做招聘，发现他们的招聘工具歧视女性，不得不关停；有些面部识别系统对浅肤色的人比对深肤色的人准确得多，如果用于刑事调查会带来严重不公；还有AI贷款审批系统对某些少数族裔给出更高利率；一个8岁女孩在搜索引擎搜CEO却只看到男性照片，这可能打消她未来追求高管职位的念头

> AI 系统今天已经在做重要决策了，未来还会做更多，所以偏见问题至关重要。举例来说，有一家公司用 AI 来做招聘，结果发现他们的招聘工具歧视女性——这显然不公平，于是这家公司关停了这个工具。另外，有些面部识别系统对浅肤色和深肤色人群的识别准确率不一样——如果 AI 系统训练数据主要是浅肤色人群，那它对这类人就会更准确。而当这些系统用于比如刑事侦查时，就可能对深肤色人群产生极具偏见和不公的影响。所以很多面部识别团队正在努力确保他们的系统不存在这种偏见。还有，有些 AI 或统计贷款审批系统最终对某些少数族裔群体给出了歧视性的更高利率。银行也在努力减少甚至消除审批系统中的这类偏见。最后，我觉得很重要的一点是：AI 系统不应该助长"强化不健康刻板印象"的有毒效应。比如，假设一个 8 岁的小女孩在图片搜索引擎里搜索"首席执行官"——如果她看到的全都只有男性照片，或者看不到任何和她性别或族裔相似的人，我们可不希望她被打击到，就此放弃将来某天成为大公司 CEO 的职业梦想。
> AI systems are already making important decisions today and will continue to do so in the future as well. So bias matters. For example, there's a company that was using AI for hiring and found that they're hiring to discriminated against women. This is clearly unfair. And so this company shut down there too. Second, there are also some facial recognition systems that seem to work more accurately for light skinned and dark skinned individuals. If an AI system is trained primarily on data of lighter skinned individuals, then it will be more accurate for that category of individuals. To the extent that these systems are used in, for example, criminal investigations, this can create a very bias and unfair effect for dark skinned individuals. So many face recognition teams today are working hard to ensure that the systems do not exhibit this type of bias. There have also been AI or statistical loan approval systems that wound up discriminating against some minority ethnic groups and quoted them a higher interest rate. Banks have also been working to make sure to diminish or eliminate this type of bias in their approval systems. Finally, I think it's important that AI systems do not contribute to the toxic effect of reinforcing unhealthy serotypes. For example, if an eight-year-old girl goes through an image search engine and searches for chief executive officer, if they see only pictures of men, or if they see no one that looks like themselves, either by gender or ethnicity, we don't want them to be discouraged from pursuing a career that might lead her to someday be a chief executive officer of a large company.

### 减少AI偏见的四种方案——第一，技术方案：当AI学到大量表示词汇的数字时，其中有少数数字对应着偏见，把那些数字"归零"就能显著降低偏见；第二，使用偏见更少、更具包容性的数据，比如做人脸识别需要包含多种族裔和性别；第三，透明度和系统性审计，比如人脸识别团队系统地检查系统在不同人群子集上的准确率，尽早发现问题并修复；第四，多元化的团队，不同视角的人更容易发现不同的问题，从源头让数据更多元、更包容

> 正因为这些问题，AI 社区在对抗偏见方面投入了大量精力。比如，我们开始在减少 AI 偏见的领域获得越来越好的技术方案。在本视频开头那个 AI 输出偏见类比的例子里——稍微简化一下——研究者发现，当 AI 学到很多用来表示词汇的数字时，其中有一小部分数字对应于偏见，如果你把那些数字"归零"、直接设为 0，偏见就会显著降低。第二个方案是尝试使用偏见更少、更包容的数据。比如，如果你在做一个面部识别系统，确保训练数据包含了多个族裔和所有性别，那么系统就会偏见更少、包容性更强。第三，很多 AI 团队正在让他们的系统接受更好的透明度和/或审计流程，这样我们可以持续检查这些 AI 系统到底有没有偏见——是哪种类型的偏见——这样至少能在大问题出现时及时发现，然后采取行动去解决。比如，很多面部识别团队正在系统性地检查他们的系统在不同人群子集上的准确性——例如深肤色和浅肤色人群的准确率差异。有了透明的系统加系统性的审计流程，就能提高我们尽早发现问题、然后进行修复的概率。最后，我认为拥有一支多元化的团队也有助于减少偏见。如果你的团队是多元化的，团队成员就更有可能发现不同类型的问题，更有可能从最开始就让你的数据更多元、更具包容性。通过在构建 AI 系统时拥有更多独特的视角，我觉得这将帮助所有人创建偏见更少的应用。
> Because of these issues, the AI community has put a lot of effort into combating bias. For example, we're starting to better and better technical solutions for reducing bias in AI systems. In the example you saw at the start of this video of the AI outputting bias analogies, simplifying the description a little bit, researchers have found that when an AI system learns a lot of different numbers with which to store words, there are a few numbers that correspond to the bias, and if you zero out those numbers, just set them to zero, then the bias diminishes significantly. A second solution is to try to use less bias and or more inclusive data. For example, if you are building a face recognition system and make sure to include data from multiple ethnicities and all genders, then your system will be less biased and more inclusive. Second, many AI teams are subjecting their systems to better transparency and or auditing processes so that we can constantly check what types of bias, if any, these AI systems are exhibiting so that we can at least recognize the problem if it exists and then take steps to address it. For example, many face recognition teams are systematically checking how accurate their system is on different subsets of the population to check whether it is more or less accurate on dark skin versus light skin individuals, for example. Having transparent systems as well as systematic auditing processes increases the odds that will at least quickly spot the problem in case there is one so we can fix it. Finally, I think having a diverse workforce will also help reduce bias. If you have a diverse workforce, then the individuals at your workforce are more likely to be able to spot different problems and maybe to help make your data more diverse and more inclusive in the first place. By having more unique points of view as you're building AI systems, I think this will help all of us create less bias applications.

### 值得乐观的是：我们今天减少AI偏见的思路，实际上比减少人类偏见的思路更清晰；虽然在任何偏见被彻底消除之前我们都不该满足，而且这需要大量工作，但如果能通过技术手段让AI系统——它们从人类那里学到了与人类同等级的偏见——在此基础上大幅降低偏见，那社会就能通过AI让决策迅速变得比纯靠人类更公平、偏见更少；接下来将讨论AI的另一大局限：对抗性攻击

> AI 系统今天在做真正重要的决策，所以它们的偏见——或者说它们的偏见风险——是我们必须关注并努力降低的。让我对此保持乐观的一点是：我们现在对减少 AI 偏见的思考，实际上比减少人类偏见的思考要更清晰。所以——虽然在任何 AI 偏见彻底消除之前我们都不该满足，而且这需要大量工作——但我也很乐观：如果我们能让 AI 系统从跟人类相近的偏见水平起步（因为它们的偏见本来就是跟人类学的），然后通过技术方案或其他手段把偏见大幅降低，那么作为一个社会，我们就有望让自己用人类或 AI 做出来的决策，迅速变得越来越公平、偏见越来越少。除了偏见问题，AI 的另一个局限是它可能暴露在对抗性攻击之下。下个视频你将会了解什么是对抗性攻击，以及你能做哪些事来防范它们。咱们进入下一个视频。
