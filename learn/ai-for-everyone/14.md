## 六、如何选择AI项目（上）——选AI项目要找"AI能做"和"业务有价值"的交集；用三个原则做头脑风暴：自动化任务而非工作、围绕业务价值驱动因素、围绕业务痛点，并且不要被"大数据"吓到，小数据集也能起步

### 选AI项目的核心框架：找两个圆的交集——左边是"AI能做的事"，右边是"对你业务有价值的事"，你要选的是落在这两个圆交集里的项目；AI专家最懂左边那个圆，业务领域专家最懂右边那个圆，所以最好把两类人聚在一起做"跨职能团队"头脑风暴

> 如果你想尝试做一个 AI 项目，怎么选一个值得做的项目呢？别指望一个好主意一夜之间冒出来——有时候会，但有时候也需要几天甚至几周才能想出值得追求的点子。这个视频给你一个框架，用来头脑风暴有潜力的 AI 项目。假设你想为你的业务做一个 AI 项目。你已经知道 AI 不是万能的，所以有一组事情是 AI 能做的——用一个圆来表示。还有一组事情是对你业务有价值的——用第二个圆来表示。你要做的就是选落在这两个圆交集里的项目——选那些既可行（AI 能做）又对你业务有价值的项目。AI 专家通常对左边那个圆里面有什么、没什么有很好的判断力，而你业务领域的专家——不管是销售、营销、农业还是别的——最清楚什么对你的业务真正有价值。所以在做头脑风暴的时候，我经常把懂 AI 的人和业务领域的专家聚到一起，让他们共同找出落在两个圆交集里的项目。我们有时也管这叫"跨职能团队"——就是同时包含 AI 专家和领域专家的团队。
> If you want to try your hand at an AI project, how do you select a worthwhile project to work on? Don't expect an idea to next become overnight. Sometimes it happens, but sometimes it also takes a few days or maybe a few weeks to come up with a worthy idea to pursue. In this video, you see a framework for brainstorming potentially exciting AI projects to pursue. Let's say you want to build an AI project for your business. You've already seen that AI can't do everything, and so there's going to be a certain set of things that is what AI can do. So let's let the circle represent the set of things that AI can do. Now there's also going to be a certain set of things that is valuable for your business. So let's let this second circle represent a set of things that are valuable for your business. What you would like to do is try to select projects that are at the intersection of these two sets. So you select projects hopefully that are both feasible, that can be done with AI, and that are also valuable for your business. So AI experts will tend to have a good sense of what is and what isn't in the set on the left, and domain experts in your business, be it sales and marketing or agriculture or something else, will have a best sense of what is actually valuable for your business. So when brainstorming projects that AI can do and are valuable for your business, I will often bring together a team comprising both people knowledgeable about AI as well as experts in your business area to brainstorm together so that together they can try to identify projects at the intersection of both of these two sets. So sometimes we also call these cross functional teams, and that just means a team that includes both AI experts as well as domain experts, meaning experts in your area of business.

### 头脑风暴的三个原则之第一：思考自动化"任务"而不是自动化"工作"——以呼叫中心为例，员工的"工作"包含接电话、回邮件、处理退款等很多任务，你要做的是从中找到某一个任务（比如来电自动路由或邮件路由）特别适合用机器学习自动化；放射科医生也同理，他们的"工作"包含读X光片、继续教育、会诊、带教、直接面对病人等，你从读X光片这一个任务切入，就能找到最有成果的AI项目

> 做头脑风暴的时候，我有一套在很多公司用过的框架，觉得很有用。分享三个原则。第一：虽然媒体大量报道 AI 会消灭工作岗位——这确实是个需要解决的重要社会问题——但在想具体的 AI 项目时，我发现想"自动化任务"比想"自动化工作"有用得多。以呼叫中心为例：呼叫中心里有很多任务——接电话、回邮件、处理具体操作比如根据客户要求退款。在这些员工做的任务里，可能有一个"来电路由"或"邮件路由"任务特别适合机器学习自动化。就是要通过审视员工做的所有任务，从中找出那一个，让你能在短期内做出最有成果的自动化项目。再看放射科医生：很多媒体报道说 AI 会取代放射科医生，但放射科医生实际上要做很多事——他们读 X 光片（这确实很重要），但他们还要继续教育、和其他医生会诊、带教年轻医生、有些还直接和病人沟通。通过看放射科医生做的所有这些任务，你才能找出其中某一个——比如读 X 光片的 AI 辅助或自动化——让你选到最有成果的项目。
> When brainstorming projects, there's a framework that I've used with a lot of companies that are found to be useful. So let me share with you three principles of three ideas for how you can have a team brainstorm projects. First, even though there's been a lot of press coverage about AI automating jobs away, and this is an important societal issue that needs to be addressed, when thinking about concrete AI projects, I find it much more useful to think about automating tasks rather than automating jobs. Take call center operations. There are a lot of tasks that happen in the call center, ranging from people picking up the phone to answering phone calls, to replying to emails, to taking specific actions, such as issuing a refund on the behalf of a customer request. But the bottom of these tasks that employees in the call center do, there may be one call routing or email routing that may be particularly amenable to machine learning automation. And it's by looking at all these tasks that the group of employees do and selecting one that may allow you to select the most fruitful project for automation in the near term. Let's look at another example, the job of radiologists. There's been a lot of press about how AI may automate radiologists' jobs, but radiologists actually do a lot of things. They read x-rays. That's really important. But they also engage in their own continuing education. They consult with other doctors. They may mentor younger doctors. Some of them also consult directly with patients. And so it's by looking at all of these tasks that a radiologist does, that you may identify one of them, let's say AI assistance or AI automation for reading x-rays, that allows you to select the most fruitful projects to work on.

### 头脑风暴三个原则之第二和第三：问"业务价值的主要驱动因素是什么"——找到能用AI或数据科学增强这些驱动因素的方式可以创造巨大价值；再问"业务的主要痛点是什么"——有些痛点可以用AI解决，有些不能，但理解这些痛点是头脑风暴的有用起点

> 我的建议是，看看你的业务，想想人们做的任务，看能不能找出其中一两个可以用机器学习自动化的。和大公司 CEO 开会做 AI 项目头脑风暴时，我常问的问题是：业务价值的主要驱动因素是什么？有时候找到 AI 或数据科学解决方案来增强这些驱动因素会非常有价值。第三个问题是：业务的主要痛点是什么？有些痛点可以用 AI 解决，有些不行，但理解业务的主要痛点也是做 AI 项目头脑风暴的一个有用起点。
> So what I would recommend is if you look at your business, think about the tasks that people do to see if you can identify just one of them or just a couple of them that may be automatable using machine learning. When meeting CEOs of large companies to brainstorm AI projects for the company, a common question I'll also ask is, what are the main drivers of business value? And sometimes finding AI solutions or data science solutions to augment this can be very valuable. Finally, a third question that I've asked is, sometimes let a valuable project idea is, what are the main pain points in your business? Some of them could be solved with AI. Some of them can't be solved with AI. But by understanding the main pain points in the business, that can create a useful starting point for brainstorming AI projects as well.

### 最后一个重要建议：没有大数据也能起步——更多人数据几乎只有好处，大数据也确实让搜索引擎这类业务拥有护城河；但大数据有时候被过度炒作了，即使只有很小的数据集你通常也能取得进展——比如做咖啡杯自动质检，你可能不希望已经生产了一百万个次品，有时候有100张、1000张甚至只有10张图片就能开始做机器学习项目；所需数据量完全取决于具体问题，跟AI工程师聊一下会更清楚，但千万别因为没有大数据就放弃

> 我还有最后一条做 AI 项目头脑风暴的建议：即使没有大数据——没有海量数据——你也可以取得进展。别误会，更多数据几乎从来不会有害，除了可能要多花点钱买磁盘空间或网络带宽来传输和存储数据。更多数据几乎永远只有好处，我也喜欢有大量数据。数据也确实让某些业务——比如网页搜索——具有防御性壁垒。网页搜索是一个"长尾"业务，意味着有大量非常非常罕见的搜索词，而看到人们在这些罕见搜索词上点击了什么，确实帮助领先的搜索引擎提供了更好的搜索体验。所以大数据很棒——当你能拿到的时候。但我认为大数据有时候也被过度炒作了。即使只有很小的数据集，你通常也能取得进展。举个例子：假设你在为咖啡杯建自动视觉检测系统，要自动检测右边的杯子有瑕疵。如果你有一百万张好杯子和次品杯子的图片，那当然很棒。但我不希望你制造了一百万个次品杯子——那代价也太高了。所以有时候只有 100 张图片，或者 1000 张，甚至有时候只有 10 张，你可能就能开始做机器学习项目了。所需数据量完全取决于具体问题，跟 AI 工程师或 AI 专家聊一下会帮你更好地判断。有些问题 1000 张图片不够，确实需要大数据才能表现好。但我的建议是：不要因为一开始没有很多数据就放弃，即使只有小数据集，你往往也能取得进展。
> I have one last piece of advice for brainstorming AI projects, which is that you can make progress even without big data, even without tons of data. Now don't get me wrong, having more data almost never hurts other than maybe needing to pay a bit more for disk space or network bandwidth to transmit and store the data. Having more data almost always is only helpful and I love having lots of data. And it is also true that data makes some businesses like web search defensible. Web search is a long tail business, meaning that there are a lot of very, very rare web queries. And so seeing what people click on when they search on all of these rare web queries does help the leading web search engines have a much better search experience. So big data is great when you can get it. But I think big data is also sometimes overhyped and even over small data set, you can still often make progress. Here's an example. Let's say you're building an automated visual inspection system for the coffee mug. So you want to automatically detect that the coffee mug on the right is defective. Well if you had a million pictures of good coffee mugs and defective coffee mugs, it would be great to have that many examples of pictures of good and bad coffee mugs to feed to your AI system. But I hope that you have not manufactured a million defective coffee mugs because that feels like a very expensive thing to have to throw away. So sometimes with as few as 100 pictures or maybe a thousand or sometimes maybe as few as 10, you may be able to get started on a machine learning project. The amount of data you need is very problem dependent and speaking with an AI engineer on AI expert would help you get a better sense. There are some problems where a thousand images may not be enough, where you do need big data to get good performance. But my advice is don't give up just because you don't have a lot of data to start off with and you can often still make progress even with a small data set.
