## 一、AI的"金凤花规则"——对AI既不能过分乐观，也不能过分悲观，取中间的务实态度刚刚好；把通用人工智能（AGI）或超级智能当作"近在眼前"是导致过度乐观和过度恐惧的共同根源

### 我们需要对AI保持一种类似"金凤花与三只熊"故事里的中庸态度——既不能过分乐观地认为AI会解决人类所有问题、带来全球乌托邦，也不能过分悲观地担心AI会有意识、成为超越人类的物种并消灭人类；后一种恐惧属于科幻小说范畴，因为人类有丰富经验控制比任何个体都强大的东西，比如公司和国家

> AI 对社会和亿万人的生活正产生巨大影响。为了让我们所有人都能做出好的决策，我们必须对 AI 抱有一个真实的认识——既不能太乐观，也不能太悲观。我说的是什么意思呢？你小时候可能读过《金凤花与三只熊》的故事——故事里说一碗粥不能太烫也不能太凉，一张床不能太硬也不能太软。我觉得我们对 AI 也需要类似的"金凤花规则"：对 AI 技术能做什么、不能做什么，我们既不应该过分乐观，也不应该过分悲观。比如，我们不应该对 AI 过分乐观。AI 是非常强大的技术，但我不指望它能解决人类所有问题，也不太可能仅凭它自己就把人类带进一个全球乌托邦。有些过度乐观源自于大家认为 AI 的"意识"、通用人工智能甚至超级智能已经近在咫尺，等到了那一天，AI 会迅速在医疗健康领域取得巨大突破，让我们活得更久更健康，还会给每个人创造难以想象的财富——我希望这能这么简单就好了。反过来，一些最悲观恐惧也跟认为超级智能近在咫尺有关——怕 AI 会产生意识，然后决定统治所有人类。在我看来这极不可能。AI 确实有风险，比如它可能产生偏见、给出不公平或不准确的输出，但"失去对 AI 的控制"到那种 AI 变成超越人来的高级物种然后消灭我们的地步，这真的属于科幻小说范畴，不是一个现实的场景。人类有丰富的经验来控制比任何个体都更强大的东西，比如公司和民族国家。AI 的输出有时不可预测，但我并不担心我们所谓的"失去对 AI 的控制"或者它变成人类的竞争对手。
> AI is having a huge impact on society and on so many people's lives. So for all of us to make good decisions, it's important that we have a realistic view of AI and be neither too optimistic nor too pessimistic. Here's what I mean. Did you ever read the story of Goldilocks and the Three Bears maybe when you were a kid? The story was that a bowl of porridge should be neither too hot nor too cold and a bed should be neither too firm nor too soft. I think we need a similar Goldilocks rule for AI where I think it's important that we be neither too optimistic nor too pessimistic about what AI technology can or cannot do. For example, we should not be too optimistic about AI technologies. AI is a very powerful technology, but I wouldn't expect it to solve all of humanity's problems and by itself usher in some sort of global utopia for humanity. Some of the over-optimism stems from people thinking that AI sentience, artificial general intelligence or even superintelligence might be right around the corner and that when we get there, AI will rapidly invent huge breakthroughs in healthcare and give us off long and healthy lives and also create an insane amount of wealth for everyone. I wish it were that easy. On the flip side, some of the most pessimistic fears about AI also relate to thinking superintelligence might be right around the corner and that AI might become sentient and decide to, I don't know, co-op all humans. This seems extremely unlikely to me. Even though AI has risks, for example, it can give bias, unfair or inaccurate outputs. Our losing control of it to the point of AI becoming akin to a superior species that then wipes us out is really in the realm of science fiction rather than a realistic scenario. Humanity has lots of experience controlling things more powerful than any individual, like corporations and nation states. And while AI's output is sometimes unpredictable, I'm not worried about us so-called losing control of AI or becoming a competitive species to us.

### 对AI的真实视角应该是：AI是非常非常强大的工具，但也有很多事情AI做不了；它有偏见、不公平、输出不准确等潜在危害，但这些是可以减轻的；它已经在创造巨大经济价值，而且在多个行业继续创造更多价值的路径出奇地清晰——AI将在可预见的未来持续帮助越来越多的人

> 我觉得对"意识"、超级智能和通用人工智能的那些不必要的恐惧和不切实际的期待，正在分散人们关注真正问题的注意力，也在社会的某些角落制造对 AI 的不必要恐慌。反过来，我认为一种更真实的 AI 视角是这样的：AI 是一个极其强大的工具，但同时也有很多 AI 做不了的事。它有一些潜在的危害，比如偏见、不公平、输出不准确——但这些都是可以缓解的。而且它已经在创造巨大的经济价值。更进一步说，我们看到了一条出奇清晰的路径，让它继续在多个行业里创造更多价值。所以我非常有信心——很多其他 AI 系统构建者也同样有信心——AI 会继续成长，在可预见的未来继续帮助越来越多的人。总结一下：与其太乐观或太悲观，"金凤花"的启示是——取中间的务实态度，刚刚好。当你和朋友聊到 AI 的时候，我希望你也能告诉他们这个 AI 的"金凤花规则"，让他们也对 AI 有一个更真实的认识。
> I think unnecessary fears and overly optimistic hopes about sentience, superintelligence, artificial general intelligence are distracting people from the real issues and is also causing unnecessary fears about AI in parts of society. In contrast, I think a more realistic view of AI is that it is a very, very powerful tool, but that there are also many things that AI cannot do. It has some potential harms like bias, unfairness and inaccurate outputs that we can mitigate, and it is already creating tremendous economic value. Further, we see a surprisingly clear path for it to continue to create even more value in multiple industries. So I'm confident and so are many other AI system builders that AI will continue to grow and continue to help more and more people for the foreseeable future. To summarize, rather than being too optimistic or too pessimistic, the lesson of Goldilocks is that taking a realistic in-between view is just right. When you speak with friends about AI, I hope you can also tell them about this Goldilocks rule for AI so that they too can have a more realistic view of AI.

### 可解释性是AI的一大局限——很多高性能AI系统是"黑箱"，它做得很好但解释不了为什么会这样做；比如一个AI系统通过X光片诊断出病人右侧气胸（右肺塌陷），虽然它通过热力图指出自己是在看右肺的某些关键特征来做判断，给了我们更多信心，但整体上AI的自我解释能力仍然很差；有意思的是，人类自己解释决策的能力其实也很差，只是因为AI是新生事物，缺乏可解释性成了它被接受的一个障碍

> AI 有很多局限。你已经看到了它在性能上的一些局限，但 AI 还有别的局限。其中一个局限是可解释性很难实现：很多高性能的 AI 系统都是"黑箱"——它运行得很好，但 AI 自己也不知道怎么解释它为什么这么做。举个例子。假设你有一个 AI 系统，让它看这张 X 光片来诊断病人有没有问题。在这个真实案例中，AI 系统说它认为病人有"右侧气胸"，也就是右肺塌陷了。但我们怎么知道 AI 说得对不对？怎么知道该不该相信 AI 的诊断？有很多工作在研究怎么让 AI 系统能够自我解释。在这个例子里，热力图是 AI 在告诉我们——它到底在看图像的哪一部分来做出这个诊断。因为它明确地基于右肺、而且事实上是基于右肺的一些关键特征来做判断，这张图会让我们更有信心，觉得 AI 的诊断是合理的。当然，公平地讲，人类自己其实也很不擅长解释我们是怎么做决策的。比如，你之前几周的课里就见过这个咖啡杯——你怎么知道这是一个咖啡杯？人类是怎么看着它就说出"这是咖啡杯"的？有些特征你可以指出来，比如有个装液体的空间、有个把手——但我们人类真的不擅长解释我们到底是怎么看着它就做出判断的。但正因为 AI 是一种相对新生的事物，缺乏可解释性就成了它被接受的一个障碍。而且，有时候如果一个 AI 系统出问题了，如果它能自我解释，也会帮我们搞清楚该怎么去改进这个系统。
> There are many limitations of AI. You have already seen earlier some of the performance limitations, but AI has other limitations as well. One of the limitations of AI is that explainability is hard, and many high-performing AI systems are black boxes, meaning that it works very well, but the AI doesn't know how to explain why it does what it does. Here's an example. Let's say you have an AI system, look at this x-ray image to diagnose if anything is wrong with the patient. In this example, which is a real example, the AI system says that it thinks the patient has right-sided pneumothorax, which means that the right lung is collapsed. But how do we know if the AI is right, and how do you know if you should trust the AI system's diagnosis or not? There's been a lot of work on making AI systems explain themselves. In this example, the heat map is the AI telling us what part of the image it is looking at in order to make this diagnosis. Because it is clearly basing this diagnosis on the right lung, and in fact on some key features of the right lung, seeing this image may give us more confidence that the AI is making a reasonable diagnosis. Now, to be fair, humans are also not very good at explaining how we make decisions ourselves. For example, you've already seen this coffee mug in the last week's videos, but how do you know it's a coffee mug? How does a human look at this and say that's a coffee mug? There are some things you can point to, like there's a room for liquid and there's a handle, but we humans are not very good at explaining how we can look at this and decide what it is. But because AI is a relatively new thing, the lack of explainability is sometimes a barrier to its acceptance. And also, sometimes if an AI system isn't working, then its ability to explain itself would also help us figure how to go in and make the AI system work better.

### 可解释性是重要的开放研究领域，实践中AI团队通常能给出"够用"的解释来让系统部署；此外AI还有偏见歧视和对抗性攻击等严重局限——如果训练数据不能反映公平价值观，AI就会学会歧视，AI社区在这些问题上取得了良好进展但还有大量工作要做

> 所以，可解释性是很多研究者正在努力攻克的重大开放研究方向之一。我在实践中看到的是，当一个 AI 团队想部署一个系统时，他们通常能找到一个"够用"的解释，让系统能够运行和部署。也就是说，可解释性虽然很难，但往往并非不可能——不过我们确实需要更好的工具来帮 AI 系统自我解释。AI 还有其他一些严重的局限。作为一个社会，我们不希望基于性别或族裔来歧视任何人，我们希望人人被公平对待。但当 AI 系统被喂进那些没能反映这些价值观的数据时，AI 就可能变得有偏见，学会歧视某些人。AI 社区正在努力攻关，也在这些问题上取得了不错的进展，但我们的路才走了一半，还有很多工作要做。你会在下个视频深入学习有偏见的 AI，也会了解一些确保你使用的 AI 系统偏见更少的方法。最后，很多 AI 系统在做经济上很重要的决策，而其中一些系统可能面临对抗性攻击——如果有人故意要来欺骗你的 AI 的话。所以，根据你的应用场景，确保你的 AI 不会暴露在对抗性攻击之下，可能非常重要。AI 与歧视、AI 与偏见，以及 AI 的对抗性攻击问题——不管对潜在的 AI 构建者和使用者，还是对整个社会，都非常重要。下个视频，我们来深入探讨 AI 与偏见的问题。
> So, explainability is one of the major open research areas that a lot of researchers are working on. What I see in practice is that when an AI team wants to deploy something, the AI team is often able to come up with an explanation that is good enough to enable the system to work and be deployed. So, explainability is hard, but is often not impossible, but we do need much better tools to help AI systems explain themselves. AI has some other serious limitations. As a society, we do not want to discriminate against individuals based on their gender, based on their ethnicity, and we want people to be treated fairly. But when AI systems are fed data that doesn't reflect these values, then an AI can become biased or can learn to discriminate against certain people. The AI community is working hard and is making good progress on these issues, but we're far from done and there's still a lot of work to do. You learn more about biased AI in the next video, and some ideas how to make sure that AI systems you work with are less biased. Finally, many AI systems are making economically important decisions, and some AI systems are open to adversarial attacks if someone else is deliberately out to fool your AI system. So, depending on your application, it may be important to make sure that you are not open to these types of attacks on your AI systems. The issues of AI and discrimination or AI and bias, as well as the issue of adversarial attacks on AI, are important both to you as a potential builder and user of AI, as well as to society.
