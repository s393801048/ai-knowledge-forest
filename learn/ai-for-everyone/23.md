### AI战略之所以放在第四步而不是第一步，是因为没有亲身体验过AI项目的公司往往定出纸上谈兵的战略——有些CEO直接把报纸标题复制过来当战略，比如说"我们的战略就是收集所有数据"，但那数据对你的公司可能根本没用；正确的做法是先做试点项目、建团队、做培训，等公司上下对AI有了真正理解后再来制定战略

> 上集讲了前三步：执行试点项目、建立内部AI团队、提供广泛AI培训。但如果你的公司不只是短期获得动能，还要长期成为一个很有价值甚至很有壁垒的公司，该怎么做呢？这集我们深入讲最后两步——AI战略和内外沟通。第四步是制定AI战略——用AI为你的行业领域创造独特的优势。这个指南有个不寻常之处：AI战略是第四步，不是第一步。当他跟很多CEO分享这个指南时，大家一致要求把战略放到第一步——"我想先搞清楚公司战略，然后找资源，然后执行"。但他发现，那些在第一步就定战略、在还没"湿过鞋"之前就试图定战略的公司，往往得出一些有时很学术化、不切实际的战略。比如有些CEO直接把报纸标题复制当战略——看到媒体说数据很重要就说"我们的战略是收集所有数据"，但那个数据对公司可能有用也可能没用。所以他强烈建议先做前面几步——执行试点项目、建一点团队、做一些培训——只有当你和你的高管团队对AI能做什么不能做什么有了稍微深入的理解之后，再来制定战略，效果会好得多。

### AI的"正向循环"（virtuous cycle）是建立竞争壁垒的核心机制——以网页搜索为例：更好的产品带来更多用户，更多用户产生更多点击数据，更多数据喂养AI引擎做出更好的产品，这个自我强化的循环让新进入者极难打破；但这不意味着小公司没机会——在还没被巨头占领的新垂直领域，你可以成为第一个启动这个正向循环的公司，比如Blue River公司先从斯坦福校园开始拿着相机去农场拍卷心菜照片、积累小数据集训练基础产品、说服农民使用后自然积累更多数据、几年后拥有了比大型科技公司还多的田间卷心菜图片数据资产，最终被John Deere以超3亿美元收购

> 在设计战略时，你还可以考虑让战略跟"AI正向循环"对齐。以网页搜索为例：为什么网页搜索是一个超级有壁垒的业务——新进入者很难跟现有大搜索引擎竞争？因为如果一个公司有更好的产品（哪怕只好一点点），就能获得更多用户；更多用户意味着你收集到更多数据——因为你观察到不同用户搜索不同词时点击了什么；这些数据又可以喂入AI引擎做出更好的产品。于是公司产品稍微好一点->用户更多->数据更多->产品更好，这个现代AI技术创造的自我强化循环让新进入者极难打破。好消息是，这个小团队也可以用这个循环来进入新的垂直市场。今天要建一个新搜索引擎跟Google/Baidu/Bing/Yandex竞争确实极难，但如果你进入一个还没有强势老玩家的全新垂直领域，你完全可以制定战略让自己成为第一个利用这个正向循环的人。举个例子：有一家公司叫Blue River，被John Deere以超3亿美元收购。他们做的是农业AI技术——造一台机器拖在拖拉机后面，在农田里给庄稼拍照，用精确AI来区分庄稼和杂草，只杀杂草不伤庄稼。吴恩达认识创始人的时候，他们还是斯坦福的学生在上他的课。他们起步时靠的就是辛苦付出——拿着自己的相机跑到农场拍了几百张庄稼和杂草的照片。起初数据集小，训练出来的基础产品也不咋样，但勉强够用了，足够说服一些农民开始用它的产品。然后这个机器在田里跑着跑着，自然就积累更多更多数据。过了几年，他们就进入了正向循环：更多数据->更好产品->更多农民使用->更多数据。几年下来，他们拥有了一个巨大的数据资产——田间卷心菜图片的数量甚至超过了大型科技公司！这让他们的业务对于那些有大量搜索数据但没有田间卷心菜图片的大公司来说都有相当强的防御壁垒。

> In addition, you might consider designing a strategy that is aligned with the virtuous cycle of AI. Let me illustrate that with an example from web search. One of the reasons that web search is a very defensible business, meaning it's very difficult for new entrants to compete with the incumbents with the existing large web search engines is this. If a company has a better product, maybe a slightly better product, then that web search engine can acquire more users. And having more users means that you collect more data because you get to observe what different users click on when you search for different terms. And that data can be fed into an AI engine to produce an even better product. So this means that the company with a somewhat better product ends up with even more users, ends up with even more data and does an even better product with this link being created by modern AI technology. And it makes it very difficult for a new entrant to break into this self-reinforcing positive feedback loop called the virtuous cycle of AI. Fortunately, though, this virtuous cycle of AI can be used by smaller teams entering new verticals as well. So I think today is very difficult to build a new web search engine to compete with Google or Baidu or Bing or Yandex. But if you are entering a new vertical, a new application area where there isn't a entrenched incumbent, then you might really develop a strategy that lets you be the one to take advantage of this virtuous cycle. Let me illustrate with an example. There is a company called Blue River that was acquired by John Deere for over 300 million US dollars. And Blue River makes agricultural technology using AI. So what they did was build these machines that would be told behind a tractor in a big agricultural field. And this machine would take pictures of crops and figure out which is a crop and which is a weed and use precision AI to kill off just the weeds but not the crop. So I knew some of the founders of Blue River while they were Stanford students taking my class. And so to get the project started, they actually just used straffiness and sweat. They used their personal cameras and went out to a bunch of farms and took a lot of pictures of crops in these agricultural fields. So they started to collect pictures of heads of cabbage and weeds around the cabbage. Once they had enough data, started off with a small data set, they could train a basic product. And the first product frankly wasn't that great. It was trained on a small data set, but it kind of worked well enough to start to convince some farmers, some users to start to use their product, to tow this machine behind the tractor in order to start killing weeds for the farmers. And once this thing was running around the farms through the process of taking pictures of heads of cabbage and killing off weeds, they naturally acquired more and more data. And over the next few years, what they did was they were able to enter this positive feedback loop where having more data allows you to have a better product, have a better product, allows you to convince more farmers to use it, and having farmers use it allows you to collect more data. And over several years, entering a virtuous cycle like this can allow you to collect a huge data asset that then makes your business quite defensible. And in fact, at the time of acquisition, I'm pretty sure that they had a much bigger data asset of pictures of heads of cabbage lying on the field than even the large tech companies had. And this actually makes the business relatively defensible from even the large tech companies that have a lot of web search data, but do not have nearly as many pictures as this company does of heads of cabbage lying in the agricultural fields.

### 大多数公司不需要也不能跟大型科技公司在AI上全面竞争，因为AI需要"行业垂直化"——你应该在自己行业里做专精的AI，而不是到处跟大公司对抗；战略要素还包括：建立数据战略（如一些面向消费者的AI公司推出免费邮件、免费照片分享等不直接变现的服务来收集数据以改善广告精准度）、建设统一数据仓库打破部门数据孤岛（50个VP管50个不同数据仓库，AI工程师就没法串联数据找到因果关系，比如制造环节的温度异常可能导致两个月后客户投诉）、利用AI加速平台网络效应（在"赢家通吃"的市场里AI可以加速用户获取帮你抢占市场）

> 很多人觉得大型科技公司很擅长AI，这是事实。但这不意味着你需要也应该在AI上全方面跟大公司竞争。因为AI很多需要针对你的行业做专门化、垂直化。对大多数公司来说，正确的做法是在你的行业领域内把AI做精做专，而不是试图在方方面面跟大公司拼AI。另外的战略要素包括：建立数据战略。一家擅长AI的公司非常善于战略性数据获取，比如一些面向消费者的大型AI公司会推出免费邮件、免费照片分享等不直接变现的服务，目的就是收集用户数据来做更精准的广告投放——变现方式跟直接收费完全不同。数据获取方式因行业而异，但吴恩达亲身经历过一种"多年棋局"——跟竞争对手玩多年的战略博弈，看谁能获取最有战略价值的数据资产。你还可以考虑建设统一数据仓库——如果你有50个VP每人管一个不同的数据仓库，AI工程师就几乎不可能把所有数据串联起来找出规律。比如制造环节的数据仓库和客户投诉的数据仓库如果完全隔离，AI工程师就没法发现"今天制造温度偏高可能导致两个月后客户投诉"这种因果关系。很多领先AI公司投入了大量前期努力把数据汇聚到一个统一的数据仓库，因为这样大大增加了工程师或软件"串联线索"发现模式的概率。最后，AI也可以用来创造网络效应和平台优势——在有"赢家通吃"特性的行业里，AI能成为巨大加速器。比如网约车平台（Uber/Lyft/Didi/Grab）有很大壁垒，因为很难同时聚集大量司机和乘客；社交媒体也是，人多平台价值大。如果你的业务有这种赢家通吃的特质，AI能帮你加速用户增长，从而大大增加你成功的概率。

> One more piece of advice. A lot of people think that some of the large tech companies are greater AI. And I think that's true. Some of the large tech companies are very good at AI. But this doesn't mean you need to or should try to compete with these large tech companies on AI in general, because a lot of AI needs to be specialized or verticalized for your industry sector. And so for most companies, they'll be in your best interest to build AI specialized for your industry and to do great work on AI for your application areas, rather than try to compete or feel like you need to compete left and right with the large tech companies on AI all over the place, which just isn't true for most companies. Other elements of an AI strategy, we are going to live in an AI powered world. And the right strategy can help your company navigate these changes much more effectively. You should also consider creating a data strategy. Living AI company is a very good at strategic data acquisition. For example, some of the large consumer facing AI companies will launch services like a free email service or a free photo sharing service or many other free services that do not monetize, but allows them to collect data in all sorts of ways that lets them learn more about you so they can serve you more relevant ads and thereby monetize the data in a way that is quite different than direct monetization of that product. The way you acquire data is very different depending on your industry vertical, but I have been involved in what feels like these multi-year chess games where other corporate competitors and I are playing multi-year games to see who can acquire the most strategic data assets. You might also consider building a unified data warehouse. If you have 50 different data warehouses under the control of 50 different vice presidents, then it's almost impossible for an AI engineer or for a piece of AI software to pull together all of this data in order to connect the dots. For example, if the data warehouse for manufacturing is in a totally different place, then the data warehouse for customer complaints, then how can an AI engineer pull together this data to figure out whether the things that might happen in manufacturing that causes you to ship a faulty cell phone that causes the customer to complain two months later. So a lot of leading AI companies have put a lot of upfront effort into pulling the data into single data warehouse because this increases the odds that an engineer or a piece of software can connect the dots and spot the patterns between how a elevated temperature in manufacturing today may result in a faulty device that leads to a customer complaint two months in the future, thus letting you go back to improve your manufacturing processes. There are many examples of this in multiple industries. You can also use AI to create network effects and platform advantages. In industries with going to take all dynamics, AI can be a huge accelerator. For example, take the right sharing or the right hailing business. Today, companies like Uber and Lyft and DDN Grab seem like they have relatively defensible businesses because there are platforms that connect drivers with passengers and it's quite difficult for a new entrant to accumulate both a large rider audience and a large passenger audience at the same time. Social media platforms like Twitter and Facebook are also very defensible because they are very strong network effects where having a lot of people on one platform makes that platform more attractive to other people. So it's very difficult for a new entrant to break in. If you are working in a business with these types of when it takes all dynamics or when it takes most dynamics, then if AI can be used to help you with growing faster, for example, with accelerating user acquisition, then that can perhaps translate into a much bigger chance that your company will be the one to succeed in this business.

> 战略是跟公司、行业、具体情况高度相关的，很难给出完全通用的建议。但希望这些原则能给你一个思考框架。AI也可以融入更传统的战略框架：比如Michael Porter提过低成本和高价值战略——如果你公司走低成本路线，可以用AI来降本；如果走高价值路线，可以用AI来提升产品价值。最后在你打造这些有价值、有壁垒的业务时，希望能做的只会让人类变得更好——AI是"超能力"，非常强大，希望你用它来让人类更好。最后一步第五步是内部和外部沟通。AI会改变公司和它的产品，跟相关利益方做好沟通很重要。这包括投资者关系——确保投资者能以AI公司的标准来合理估值你的公司；也包括政府关系——比如AI进入医疗行业，这是一个高度监管的行业，政府有正当需求保护患者利益，所以公司需要跟政府沟通、做公私合作，确保AI方案能带来好处同时政府也能保护消费者和患者。自动驾驶和金融也一样。如果你的产品变了，消费者或用户教育也很重要。AI人才当今极其稀缺，如果你能展示一些初期成功案例，对招聘极有帮助。内部沟通也很重要——如果公司在做转型，内部很多人可能会有担忧，有些合理有些不那么理性，做好内部沟通来安抚人心总是有好处的。通过这五步，希望能给你一个清晰的蓝图，帮你帮一家公司真正擅长AI。接下来他还见过很多公司在AI落地上踩过的坑，下个视频来讲怎么避开这些常见陷阱。
