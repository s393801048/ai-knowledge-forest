## 五、人工智能公司的核心要素——用几个神经网络不叫AI公司，真正的AI公司是系统性做好AI让你能做的事：战略数据获取、统一数据仓库、发现自动化机会、引入新角色，以及一套五步AI转型指南

### 互联网时代教给我们一个重要教训：给你最爱的购物中心建个网站、在上面卖东西，这本身并不把它变成互联网公司；真正的互联网公司会做那些"互联网让你能做的事"——比如普及A/B测试（随时上线两个版本看哪个更好，传统购物中心可没法在平行宇宙开两家店），迭代周期极短（每周甚至每天上新功能，而购物中心改造一次要几个月），以及把决策权下放给工程师和产品经理（而不是CEO一个人拍板——因为只有工程师和产品经理才足够了解技术、产品和用户）

> 什么让一家公司擅长AI？也许更重要的是，你的公司要怎么样才能变得擅长用AI？我之前领导过Google Brain团队和百度的AI团队，分别帮Google和百度成为了优秀的AI公司。那你能为自己的公司做什么呢？我从互联网的崛起中学到了一个教训，我认为这对所有人在AI崛起中找准方向都很重要。来看看。互联网时代教给我们的一个教训是：如果你拿你最喜欢的购物中心——比如我和我太太有时候去Stanford购物中心逛街——你给它建一个网站，在上面卖东西，这本身并不把这个购物中心变成一家互联网公司。事实上，几年前我跟一家大型零售公司的CEO聊天，他对我说：安德鲁，我有个网站，我在网站上卖东西；亚马逊也有个网站，亚马逊也在网站上卖东西——一样的嘛。但当然不一样。有网站的购物中心跟一流的互联网公司根本不是一回事。那到底是什么定义了一家互联网公司，如果不是"是否在网站上卖东西？"我认为互联网公司是那些"把互联网让你能做的事做得特别好"的公司。比如，互联网公司进行普及化的A/B测试——也就是说我们习惯性地同时上线两个不同版本的网站，看哪个效果更好，因为我们能做得到——这样我们学习的速度快得多。而传统购物中心很难在两个平行宇宙同时运营两家店，你可能只能每个季度或每半年做一些改动。互联网公司的迭代周期往往非常短，你可以每周甚至每天发布新功能——因为你能做得到。而购物中心只能每隔几个月重新设计和改造一次。互联网公司也倾向于把决策权从CEO下放到工程师和其他专业角色比如产品经理手中。这跟传统购物中心形成对比——传统模式是CEO拍板所有关键决策，然后所有人按CEO说的做。结果证明传统模式在互联网时代行不通，因为只有工程师和产品经理这类专业角色才足够了解技术、产品和用户，才能做出好决策。所以这些就是互联网公司为了"把互联网让你能做的事做好"而做的事。这是从互联网时代学到的教训。

> A lesson we learned from the rise of the internet was that if you take your favorite shopping mall, so my wife and I sometimes shop at Stanford Shopping Center, and you build a website for the shopping mall, maybe sell things on the website, that by itself does not turn the shopping mall into an internet company. In fact, a few years ago, I was speaking with the CEO of a large retail company who said to me, hey Andrew, I have a website, I sell things on the website, Amazon has a website, Amazon sells things on the website is the same thing. But of course it wasn't, and the shopping mall with a website isn't the same thing as a first class internet company. So what is it that defines an internet company if it isn't just whether or not you sell things on the website? I think an internet company is a company that does the things the internet lets you do really well. For example, we engage in pervasive A/B testing, meaning we routinely throw up two different versions of the website and see which one works better because we can, and so we learn much faster. Whereas in a traditional shopping mall, it's very difficult to have two shopping malls in two parallel universes, and you can only maybe change things around every quarter or every six months. Internet companies tend to have very short iteration times. You can ship a new product every week or maybe even every day because you can. Whereas a shopping mall can be redesigned and re-architected only every several months. Internet companies also tend to push decision making down from the CEO to the engineers and to other specialized roles such as the product managers. This is in contrast to a traditional shopping mall where you can maybe have the CEO just decide all the key decisions and then just everyone does what the CEO says. And it turns out that traditional model doesn't work in the internet era because only the engineers and other specialized roles like product managers know enough about the technology and the product and the users to make great decisions. So these are some of the things that internet companies do in order to make sure they do the things that the internet lets them do really well. This is a lesson we learned from the internet era.

### AI时代同理：今天你可以让任何公司用几个神经网络或深度学习算法，但这本身并不会把它变成AI公司；真正的AI公司（有时候叫"AI优先公司"）是那些"把AI让你能做的事做得特别好"的公司——比如战略数据获取，很多大型消费科技公司会推出不赚钱的免费产品来获取数据，然后在别的地方变现，吴恩达亲自带过战略团队，专门推出不盈利的产品仅仅为了获取数据

> AI时代呢？我认为，今天你可以让任何公司用几个神经网络或几个深度学习算法，但这本身并不会把公司变成AI公司。相反，是什么造就了一家优秀的AI公司——有时候叫"AI优先公司"——是你是不是在"把AI让你能做的事做得特别好"？比如，AI公司非常擅长战略数据获取。这就是为什么很多大型消费科技公司会有不赚钱的免费产品——因为这些产品让他们能获取数据，然后在别的地方变现。我亲自带过战略团队，我们会有意推出完全不赚钱的产品，目的仅仅是为了获取数据。思考"怎么获取数据"是优秀AI公司的关键能力之一。

> How about the AI era? I think that today you can take any company and have it use a few neural networks or a few deep learning algorithms that by itself does not turn the company into an AI company. Instead what makes a great AI company sometimes an AI first company is are you doing the things that AI lets you do really well? For example, AI companies are very good at strategic data acquisition. This is why many of the large consumer tech companies may have free products that do not monetize and it allows them to acquire data that they can monetize elsewhere. So let strategy teams where we would deliberately launch products that do not make any money just for the sake of data acquisition. And thinking through how to get data is a key part of the great AI companies.

### AI公司往往拥有统一的数据仓库——如果你有50个不同的数据库或数据仓库分别由50个不同副总裁掌控，那工程师根本没法把数据汇聚起来"连点成线"发现规律；所以很多优秀的AI公司会提前投资，把数据汇集到单一数据仓库中，增加团队把不同线索串联起来的概率，当然前提是遵守隐私保护和GDPR等数据法规

> AI公司往往拥有统一的数据仓库。如果你有50个不同的数据库或50个不同的数据仓库，分别由50个不同副总裁掌管，那工程师根本不可能把数据汇聚到一个地方去"连点成线"、发现规律。所以很多优秀的AI公司会提前投资，把数据汇集到单一数据仓库中，以增加团队能把不同线索串联起来的概率。当然这一切的前提是遵守隐私保护以及像欧洲GDPR这样的数据法规。

> AI companies tend to have unified data warehouses. If you have 50 different databases or 50 different data warehouses under the control of 50 different vice presidents, then it will be impossible for an engineer to get the data into one place so that they can connect the dots and spot the patterns. So many great AI companies have preemptively invested in bringing the data together into single data warehouse to increase the odds that the teams can connect the dots. Subjective course to privacy guarantees and also to data regulations such as GDPR in Europe.

### AI公司还擅长发现自动化机会——善于判断"这里可以塞进一个监督学习算法的A到B映射，让人不用再做这个任务，而是自动完成"；AI公司也引入了很多新角色，比如机器学习工程师（MLE），以及划分团队任务的新方式；简单说，让公司擅长AI，就是把公司架构设计成"把AI让你能做的事做好"的样子

> AI公司非常擅长发现自动化机会——我们很善于发现：哦，这里可以插入一个监督学习算法，用一个A到B的映射来搞定，这样就不需要人来做这些任务了，我们可以自动化它。AI公司也有很多新角色，比如MLE——机器学习工程师——以及把任务拆分给团队成员的新方式。所以，要让你公司擅长AI，意味着要把公司的架构设计成"把AI让你能做的事做好"的样子。

> AI companies are very good at spotting automation opportunities. We're very good at seeing, oh, let's insert a supervised learning algorithm and have an A to B mapping here so that we don't have to have people do these tasks instead we can automate it. AI companies also have many new roles such as the MLE or machine learning engineer and new ways of dividing up tasks among different members of a team. So for a company to become good at AI means architecting the company to do the things that AI makes it possible to do really well.

### 让公司变得擅长AI需要一个过程，这不是什么神秘魔法——十年前Google、百度、Facebook、微软也不是今天这样的AI公司；吴恩达提出了一套系统化的"五步AI转型指南"：第一步，执行试点项目来获得动力，先做几个小项目感受AI能做什么、做AI项目是什么体验，可以在内部做也可以外包；第二步，建立内部AI团队并提供广泛的AI培训——不只是培训工程师，还要培训经理、部门负责人和高管，教会他们如何思考AI

> 让一家公司变得擅长AI确实需要一个过程。事实上，十年前，Google和百度——以及像Facebook和微软这样的公司——也并不是今天这样的优秀AI公司。那一家公司怎么变得擅长AI呢？事实证明，变得擅长AI并不是什么神秘的魔法过程。相反，有一套系统化的流程，几乎任何大公司都可以通过它变得擅长AI。这就是我推荐给那些想有效使用AI的公司的"五步AI转型指南"。我先在这里做一个简要概述，后面某一周会详细展开。第一步：执行试点项目来获得动力。就是先做几个小项目，更好地感受AI能做什么、不能做什么，以及做AI项目到底是什么体验。这可以在内部做，也可以外包团队来做。但最终你需要进入第二步：建立内部AI团队，并提供广泛的AI培训——不只是培训工程师，还要培训经理、部门负责人和高管，教会他们如何思考AI。

But eventually you then need to do step two, which is to build an in-house AI team and provide broad AI training, not just to the engineers, but also to the managers, division leaders and executives on how to think about AI.
