## 七、如何选择AI项目（下）——确定项目前要做技术尽调和业务尽调；还要加一道伦理尽调确保对人类社会有益；另外要面对"自建还是采购"的经典选择，记住"别在火车前面跑"

### 在投入一个可能需要好几个月的大项目之前，要做"尽职调查"——技术尽调要问三个问题：AI系统能否达到期望的性能水平（比如语音识别95%准确率或视觉检测99%准确率是否可行）？需要多少数据才能达到这个性能、你有没有办法获取那么多数据？工程时间线——需要多长时间、多少人才能建成？

> 也许你有一堆 AI 项目的想法，但在确定投入之前，你怎么确保这真的是一个值得做的项目？如果是个小项目，几天就能搞定，可能直接跳进去试试看就行了。但有些 AI 项目可能要几个月才能完成。这个视频我想带你走一遍我用来验证一个项目值不值得花好几个月投入的流程。在投入一个大 AI 项目之前，我通常会做尽职调查。法律世界里"尽职调查"有特定含义，但通俗地说就是你花一些时间去确保你希望是真的东西确实是真的。你已经知道最好的 AI 项目是那些既可行（AI 能做）又有价值的——我们要选落在两个圆交集里的项目。所以为了确保项目可行，我会做技术尽调；为了确保项目有价值，我会做业务尽调。技术尽调是确保你希望建的 AI 系统真的能做出来。你可以跟 AI 专家聊聊这个 AI 系统能不能达到你期望的性能水平——比如你想建一个 95% 准确率的语音识别系统，或者一个 99% 准确率的工厂咖啡杯检测系统，这在今天的技术下真的能做到吗？技术尽调的第二个重要问题是：要达到这个性能水平需要多少数据，你有没有办法获取那么多数据？第三个问题是工程时间线——大概需要多长时间、多少人才能建成你想要的系统。
> Maybe you have a lot of ideas for possible AI projects to work on, but before committing to one, how can you make sure that this really is a worthwhile project? It is a quick project that might take you just a few days, maybe just jump in right away and see if it works or not, but some AI projects may take many months to execute. In this video, I want to step you through the process that I use to double check that a project is worth that many months of effort. Let's take a look. Before committing to a big AI project, I will usually conduct due diligence on it. Due diligence has a specific meaning in the legal world, but informally it just means that you want to spend some time to make sure what your hope is true really is true. You've already seen how the best AI projects are ones that are feasible, so it's something that AI can do as well as valuable. We really want to choose projects at the intersection of these two sets. So to make sure a project is feasible, I will usually go through technical diligence and to make sure that the project is valuable, I will usually go through a business diligence process. Technical diligence is the process of making sure that the AI system you hope to build really is doable, really is feasible. So you might talk to AI experts about whether or not the AI system can actually meet the desired level of performance. For example, if you are hoping to build a speech system that is 95% accurate, consulting with AI experts or perhaps reading some of the trade literature can give you a sense of whether this is doable or not. Or if you want a system to inspect coffee mugs in the factory and you need your system to be 99% accurate, again, is this actually doable with today's technology? A second important question for technical diligence is how much data is needed to get to this desired level of performance and do you have a way to get that much data? Third would be engineering timeline to try to figure out how long it will take and how many people it will take to build the system that you would like to have built.

### 业务尽调要仔细想清楚：这个AI系统建出来之后——比如一个95%准确率的语音识别系统或99.9%准确率的视觉检测系统——能不能帮你实现业务目标？无论是改善现有业务还是创造全新业务线；吴恩达通常会建财务模型来定量估算：到底能省多少钱、增加多少收入，把项目的经济效益建模出来再决定是否投入好几个月

> 除了技术尽调，我通常还会做业务尽调，确保你设想的项目真的对业务有价值。很多 AI 项目通过降低成本来创造价值——比如自动化几个任务或挤出系统效率。很多 AI 系统也能增加收入——比如让更多人完成购物车结账。或者你建的 AI 系统是为了推出新产品或新业务线。所以业务尽调就是仔细想清楚：你正在建的 AI 系统——比如一个 95% 准确率的语音识别系统或 99.9% 准确率的视觉检测系统——能不能帮你实现业务目标？不管你的业务目标是改善现有业务还是创造全新业务线。做业务尽调时，我通常会建电子表格财务模型来定量估算价值——比如估算实际能省多少钱，或者合理的收入增长假设是什么，把项目的经济学模型建出来，再决定是否投入好几个月去做。
> In addition to technical diligence, I will often also conduct business diligence to make sure that the project you envision really is valuable for the business. So a lot of AI projects will drive value through lowering costs, for example, by automating a few tasks or by squeezing more efficiency out of the system. A lot of AI systems can also increase revenue, for example, driving more people to check out in your shopping cart. Or you may be building an AI system to help you launch a new product or a new line of business. So business diligence is the process of thinking through carefully for the AI system that you're building, such as a speech recognition system that's 95% accurate or a visual inspection system that's 99.9% accurate, will allow you to achieve your business goals. Whether your business goal is to improve your current business or to even create brand new businesses in your company. When conducting business diligence, I'll often end up building spreadsheet financial models to estimate the value quantitatively, such as estimate how many dollars are actually saved or what do we think is a reasonable assumption in terms of entries revenue and to model out the economics associated with the project before committing to many months of effort on a project.

### 除了技术尽调和业务尽调，还有第三道"伦理尽调"——AI能做很多能赚钱的事，但有些事赚了钱却不能让社会变得更好；在确定项目之前，请确保你做的事对人类社会是有益的（第四周会详细讨论）

> 虽然这张幻灯片上没有明确列出来，但有一件事我希望你也能做：第三类尽调——伦理尽调。我认为 AI 有很多能做的事，有的甚至能赚很多钱，但可能不会让社会变得更好。所以除了技术尽调和业务尽调，我希望你也能做伦理尽调，确保你做的事真正让人类和社会变得更好。我们会在课程最后一周详细讨论这个。
> Although not explicitly listed on this slide, one thing I hope you also consider doing is a third type of diligence, which is ethical diligence. I think there are a lot of things that AI can do that will even make a lot of money, but that may not make society better off. So in addition to technical diligence and business diligence, I hope you also conduct ethical diligence and make sure that whatever you're doing is actually making humanity and making society better off. ### 自建还是采购？这是IT界的经典问题——机器学习项目可以自己做也可以外包，两种模式都有成功案例，外包能更快获得人才和启动速度，但最终建起自己的内部AI团队更好；数据科学项目则更多在内部做，因为它和你的业务绑定太深，需要对业务有非常深入的日常理解；记住"别在火车前面跑"——如果某样东西正在成为行业标准（可能是一家创业公司、大公司或开源项目在推动），你不如拥抱行业标准而不是自己从头造，因为你的资源有限，应该聚焦在最独特、最能对你公司产生差异化价值的项目上

> 规划 AI 项目时你还要决定：自建还是采购？这是 IT 界的老问题了，AI 领域也面临同样的选择。比如现在几乎没有公司自己造电脑了，大家都买别人的电脑；几乎没有公司自己造 Wi-Fi 路由器了，大家都买商用路由器。机器学习项目可以内部做也可以外包——两种模式我都见过成功的。有时候外包可以更快获得人才、项目启动更快。但如果最终能建起自己的内部 AI 团队来做这些项目，那会更好——下周讲 AI 转型指南时会详细说。和数据科学项目不像机器学习项目，它们更多是在内部做的。不是不可能外包，有时候也能外包。但我的观察是数据科学项目和你的业务绑定太深，需要对你业务非常深入的日常理解才能做出最好的数据科学项目。所以按比例来说，我见到内部做的数据科学项目比机器学习项目更多。最后，每个行业里有些东西会成为行业标准，你应该避免自己去做那些。对于"自建vs采购"的一个常见答案是：建那些对你来说非常专有、完全定制化的，或者能让你建立独特防御壁垒或竞争优势的东西。而那些会成为行业标准的东西，很可能其他公司会做，你去买比自己做更高效。我的一个团队有句话说得很好："别在火车前面跑"。意思是——如果这是铁轨上跑的火车，前面冒着烟——你要做的不是拼命比火车跑得更快。火车就是行业标准解决方案。如果有公司——可能是创业公司、大公司或者开源项目——正在建一个行业标准解决方案，那你可能应该避免拼命跑在火车前面。因为就算你短期内能跑得更快，最终火车会追上来——在火车前面跑的人会被碾过去。所以当有一个行业标准解决方案被大规模推进的时候，你不如拥抱这个标准或拥抱别人的平台，而不是试图所有东西都在内部自己造。我们都活在一个资源有限的世界里——有限的时间、有限的数据、有限的工程资源。所以我希望你能把这些资源聚焦在最独特、能对你公司产生最大差异化影响的项目上。
> As you're planning out your AI project, you also have to decide do you want to build or buy? This is an age old question in the IT world, and we're facing this question in AI as well. For example, hardly any companies build their own computers these days. They buy someone else's computers and hardly any companies build their own Wi-Fi routers just buy a commercial Wi-Fi router. How about machine learning and data science? Machine learning projects can be in-house or outsourced. I've seen both of these models use successfully. Sometimes if you outsource a machine learning project, you can have access much more quickly to talent and get going faster on a project. It is nice if eventually you build your own in-house AI team and can also do these projects in-house. You hear more about this when we talk about the AI Transformation Playbook in greater detail next week. Unlike machine learning projects though, data science projects are more commonly done in-house. They're not impossible to outsource. You can sometimes outsource them. But what I've seen is that data science projects are often so closely tied to your business that it takes very deep day-to-day knowledge about your business to do the best data science projects. And so just as a percentage as a fraction, I see data science projects in-house more than machine learning projects. Finally, in every industry, some things will be industry standard and you should avoid building those. A common answer to the build versus buy question was build the things that are going to be quite specialized to you or completely specialized to you or that allow you to build a unique defense or advantage. But the things that will be industry standard, probably some other company will build and it'll be more efficient for you to just buy it rather than build it in-house. One of my teams that I really poetic phrase, which is don't sprint in front of a train. And what that means is if this is a train running on the railway tracks, and there's the la chimney with the puff of smoke, what you don't want to do is to be the person or the engineer trying to sprint faster and faster ahead of the train. The train is the industry standard solution. And so if there's a company, maybe a startup, maybe a big company, or maybe an open source effort that is building an industry standard solution, then you may want to avoid trying to run faster and faster to keep ahead of the train. Because even though you could sprint faster in the short term, eventually the train will catch up and you know crush someone trying to sprint in front of a train. So when there's a massive force of an industry standard solution that is being built, you might be better off just embracing an industry standard or embracing someone else's platform rather than trying to do everything in-house. We all live in a world of limited resources, limited time, limited data, limited engineering resources. And so I hope you can focus those resources on the projects that are most unique and will make the biggest difference to your company.
