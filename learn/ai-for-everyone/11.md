## 三、机器学习项目工作流程——机器学习项目有三个核心步骤：收集数据、训练模型、部署模型，整个过程充满迭代，以语音识别和自动驾驶为例一步步拆解给你看

### 机器学习项目的工作流分三步：第一步是收集数据——以语音识别为例，你想让系统识别"Alexa"这个唤醒词，你就得去录一堆人说"Alexa"的音频，再录一堆人说其他词比如"hello"的音频，把这些音频收集起来；第二步是用机器学习算法来学习从输入（音频）到输出（说的是什么词）的A到B映射

> 机器学习算法可以学习从输入到输出、或者说从 A 到 B 的映射。那你怎么构建一个机器学习项目呢？这个视频你会学到机器学习项目的工作流程。咱们用语音识别来举例。你们家可能已经有 Amazon Echo、Google Home、Apple Siri 或者百度 DuerOS 设备了。几年前我在 Google 的语音识别系统上做过一些工作，也主导了百度的 DuerOS 项目。今天我自己厨房里就有一个 Amazon Echo。每次我煮鸡蛋的时候都会说："Alexa，设个三分钟计时"，然后它会在三分钟到了的时候提醒我，鸡蛋煮好了。那你怎么构建一个能识别"Alexa"或"Hey Google"或"Hey Siri"或"你好小度"的语音识别系统呢？咱们来梳理关键步骤。为了简单起见，我用 Amazon Echo 检测"Alexa"关键词作为例子。第一步是收集数据——你去找一些人说"Alexa"这个词，把音频录下来；同时也找一堆人说其他词，比如"hello"，也录下来。第二步是训练模型，用机器学习算法学习 A 到 B 的映射，其中输入 A 是一段音频，第一段音频系统应该输出"用户说了 Alexa"，第二段音频输出"用户说了 hello"。每次 AI 团队开始训练模型时，第一次尝试几乎总是不太好用，团队需要反复尝试很多次——在 AI 领域我们叫"迭代"——直到模型表现足够好为止。
> Machine learning algorithms can learn input to output or A to B mappings. So how do you build a machine learning project? In this video you'll learn what is the workflow of machine learning projects. Let's take a look. As a running example, I'm going to use speech recognition. So some of you may have an Amazon Echo or Google Home or Apple Siri device or a Baidu Duo OS device in your homes. So how do you build a speech recognition system that can recognize when you say, Alexa or hey Google or hey Siri or hello Baidu? Let's go through the key steps of a machine learning project. And just for simplicity, I'm going to use Amazon Echo or detecting the Alexa keyword as this running example. The first step is to collect data. So that means you go around and get some people to say the word Alexa for you and you record the audio of that. And you'll also get a bunch of people to say other words like hello or say lots of other words and record the audio of that as well. Having collected a lot of audio data, a lot of these audio clips of people saying either Alexa or saying other things, step two is to then train the model. And this means you would use a machine learning algorithm to learn an input to output or A to B mapping where the input A would be an audio clip. And in the case of the first audio clip above, hopefully it will tell you that the user said Alexa and in the case of audio clip two shown on the right, hopefully the system will learn to recognize that the user has said hello. Whenever an AI team starts to train the model, meaning to learn the A to B or input output mapping, what happens pretty much every time is the first attempt doesn't work well. And so invariably the team will need to try many times or in AI we call this iterate many times. You have to iterate many times until hopefully the model looks like it's good enough.

### 第三步是部署模型——把AI软件放进实际的智能音箱里，交付给测试用户或大规模用户群；很多AI产品上线后你会发现，在实际环境里模型表现可能不如预期，比如你拿美国口音训练的系统发到英国，英国人一说"Alexa"系统就懵了，这时你需要获取这些新数据回来维护和更新模型

> 第三步是部署模型，也就是把这个 AI 软件放进实际的智能音箱里，交付给一小批测试用户或者大规模用户群。很多 AI 产品上线后你会发现，当它开始接收新数据时，表现可能不如你最初期望的那么好。比如说我是英国人，就拿英国人开涮吧——假设你的语音识别系统是用美国口音训练的，然后你把这个智能音箱发到英国，英国人开始说"Alexa"，你可能发现它识别英国口音的语音不如你期望的好。当这种情况发生时，希望你能获取到这些英国口音表现不佳的数据，然后用来维护和更新模型。总结一下，机器学习项目的关键步骤是：收集数据、训练模型（A 到 B 的映射）、部署模型。贯穿这些步骤的是大量的迭代——微调、适配模型让它更好用，甚至在发货后获取数据回来持续改进产品，当然这取决于你能不能拿到数据回传。
> The third step is to then actually deploy the model. And what that means is you put this AI software into an actual smart speaker and ship it to either a small group of test users or to a large group of users. What happens in a lot of AI products is that when you ship it, you see that it starts getting new data and it may not work as well as you had initially hoped. So for example, I am from the UK, so I'm going to pick on the British, but let's say you had trained your speech recognition system on American accented speakers and you then ship this smart speaker to the UK and you start having British accented people say Alexa, then you may find that it doesn't recognize this speech as well as you had hoped. And when that happens, hopefully you can get data back of cases such as maybe British accented speakers was not working as well as you're hoping and it uses data to maintain and to update the model. So to summarize, the key steps of a machine learning project are to collect data, to train the model, the A to B mapping, and then to deploy the model. And throughout these steps, there is often a lot of iteration, meaning fine tuning or adapting the model to work better or getting data back even after you've shipped it to hopefully make the product better, which may or may not be possible depending on whether you're able to get data back.
