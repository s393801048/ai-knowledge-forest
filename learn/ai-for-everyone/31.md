## 一、对抗性攻击——人类肉眼几乎看不出任何变化的微小像素扰动，就能让AI把蜂鸟认成锤子、把野兔认成桌子、把香蕉认成烤面包机，甚至贴上贴纸就能让自动驾驶汽车看不见停车标志

### 现代AI系统有时会被"骗"——给一张鸟的图片，AI正确识别为蜂鸟；但在图片上做一个肉眼几乎察觉不到的微小像素改变后，同一个AI系统却说这是一把锤子；这就是对抗性攻击，即有人故意去欺骗AI系统，让它做出本不该做的错误判断；AI"看"世界的方式跟人类完全不同，所以它对人类察觉不到的改动却可能极其敏感

> 尽管现代 AI 极其强大，但现代 AI 技术——尤其是深度学习——的一个局限是：有时它会"被骗"。具体来说，如果有人故意要欺骗你的 AI 系统，现代 AI 有时会容易遭受"对抗性攻击"。我们来看一下。假设你给 AI 系统这张鸟的图片，让它做分类。AI 系统输出说这是一只蜂鸟。但是，我们对这张图片做一个微小的扰动——我说的"微小扰动"意思是把像素值改动一点点，大多数人都察觉不到这种变化。同样的 AI 系统却说——这是一把锤子。你可能会说，这怎么可能？右边的图看起来跟左边几乎一模一样。事实上这些变化对人眼来说几乎是察觉不到的。但 AI 系统"看"世界的方式跟你我非常不同。因此，如果一个攻击者对图片做出了你我看不出、却能让 AI 误以为图片内容完全不同的改动，AI 就很容易上当。我们把这叫做对 AI 系统的"对抗性攻击"。在计算机安全领域，"攻击安全系统"的意思是试图让系统做它本不该做的事。同理，对 AI 系统的对抗性攻击，就是试图让它做它本不该做的事——比如骗它输出错误的分类结果。还有一个例子：这是一张野兔的图片——只需对像素值做微小改动——AI 却说这是一张桌子。计算机看图片的方式和人类不同，这既有好处也有坏处。比如，计算机系统读条形码和二维码比你我强得多。但深度学习系统的工作方式，也让它们对这些特定形式的攻击打开大门——而这样的攻击没有人类会上当。
> Even though modern AI is incredibly powerful, one of the limitations of modern AI technologies, especially deep learning, is that sometimes it can be fooled. In particular, modern AI systems are sometimes susceptible to adversarial attacks if someone else sets out deliberately to fool your AI system. Let's take a look. Let's say you give an AI system this picture of a bird and ask it to classify it. The AI system outputs that this is a hummingbird. But let's make a minor perturbation to this image. By minor perturbation, I mean to change the pixel values just a little bit, an almost imperceptible change to most people. The same AI system then says this is instead a hammer. Now to a person, you might say, how is this even possible? The picture on the right looks almost identical to the picture on the left. And in fact, the changes are almost imperceptible to the human eye. But an AI system sees the world very differently than you and I do. And so it is susceptible to, if an adversary makes changes to a picture that could be imperceptible to you and me, but their results in fooling the AI into thinking this picture is something totally different. We call this an adversarial attack on an AI system. In computer security, an attack against a secure system means an attempt to make it do something other than what it was intended to do. In the same way, an adversarial attack on an AI system is an attempt to make it do something other than what it was intended to do, such as trying to fool it into outputting incorrect classifications. Here's another example. Here's a picture of a hare with just a minor perturbation or a small change to the pixel values. AI instead says this is a desk. The fact that computers see pictures differently than humans do gives it advantages and disadvantages. For example, computer systems are much better than you and me at reading barcodes and QR codes. But the way that deep learning systems work also opens it up to these particular forms of attack, which no human would be fooled by.

### 攻击不仅能发生在数字图片上，还能延伸到物理世界——卡内基梅隆大学研究者设计了一副古怪的眼镜，戴上后能骗过AI系统让它以为这个男人是女演员米拉·乔沃维奇；UC伯克利和密歇根大学等研究者展示了在停车标志上贴贴纸，能让AI完全"看不见"停车标志——人类依然轻松看到这是个停车标志，但如果用在自动驾驶汽车上就非常危险

> 今天，AI 被用来过滤垃圾邮件、过滤仇恨言论——而像这样的攻击会削弱这些过滤器的效果。目前这页幻灯片里的攻击需要直接修改图像的能力——比如垃圾邮件发送者在把图片上传到网站或放进邮件之前直接修改它。还有一些攻击则通过改变物理世界来实现。例如，卡内基梅隆大学的一个团队设计了一副像这样很"搞怪"的眼镜——当这位男士戴上这副眼镜后，他能骗过 AI 系统让 AI 以为他是女演员米拉·乔沃维奇。我觉得这真的很惊人——只要戴这么一副眼镜，就能骗 AI 以为这个男人是一位著名女演员。另一组来自 UC 伯克利、密歇根大学等高校的研究者展示：如果你把像这样的贴纸贴在停车标志上，可以让 AI 系统完全"看不见"这个停车标志。这个例子特别有意思的一点是——它看起来就像有人在停车标志上涂了个鸦，大多数人类依然能很轻松地认出这是停车标志。但如果你的自动驾驶汽车里内置了计算机视觉系统，而因为贴了这些贴纸，车就不认识停车标志了——那就非常糟糕了。
> Today, AI is being used to filter out spam, to try to filter out hate speech, and attacks like these will diminish the effectiveness of such filters. Now the attacks on this slide require the ability to modify an image directly. For example, a spammer might directly modify an image before they try to upload it to a website or send it in an email. There are some attacks that work by changing the physical world as well. For example, a group at Carnegie Mellon University was able to design a funky pair of glasses like this so that when this man wears this pair of glasses, he can fool an AI system into thinking that he is actress Mila Jovovich. I think it's remarkable that just wearing a pair of glasses like this can fool an AI system into thinking that this man is a well-known actress. A different group of researchers from UC Berkeley, University of Michigan, and other universities showed that if you affix stickers like these onto a stop sign, you can fool an AI system into not seeing the stop sign at all. One interesting thing about this example is that it looks like the stop sign just had some graffiti applied on top of it and most humans would still see this as a stop sign quite easily. But if you have a computer vision system built into a self-driving car, for example, it would be really unfortunate if the car doesn't see the stop sign anymore because of these stickers applied on top of it.

### Google研究者展示了另一个惊人案例：给AI看一张香蕉图片，AI正常识别为香蕉；但当在场景中放入一张特殊设计的贴纸后，AI几乎百分之百确信这张图片里是一个烤面包机——研究人员甚至把贴纸图案公开发表在论文中，意味着理论上任何人都能下载论文、打印贴纸，去欺骗AI系统

> 最后一个例子来自 Google 的一组研究者：如果你给 AI 系统看这张图，它会说这是香蕉。但研究者设计了一种贴纸——把它放进场景中——就能让 AI 把香蕉错分。我给你看看研究者做的视频。左边是分类器的输入，右边是分类器的输出——它认为很可能是香蕉，还有很小概率是一只蛞蝓。看起来还行。来看看放入贴纸后会发生什么。当这个贴纸被放入场景后，AI 系统几乎百分之百确信这张图是烤面包机。这项研究有一个很有意思的地方：幻灯片底部引用的这篇论文的作者，竟然在论文里公开发表了他们贴纸的图片——这意味着理论上海任何人都可以下载论文、把贴纸打印出来，贴到某个地方来骗 AI 系统以为那里有个烤面包机。当然，我绝对不支持任何人去攻击 AI 系统骗它们以为某处有烤面包机。但这恰恰说明了——不幸的是——攻击这些 AI 系统可能有多么容易。
> One last example, this one from a group of researchers at Google, is if you show an AI system this picture, it will say this is a banana. But what the researchers did was design a sticker so that if you place it into the scene, it misclassifies this banana. Let me show you the video that the researchers made. Shown on the left is the classifier input and shown on the right is the classifier output where it thinks it's very likely a banana and maybe this is a small chance is a slug. Looks okay. Let's see what happens when you put a sticker or put a small patch into the scene. When the sticker is placed in the scene, the AI system now is all but certain that this picture is a picture of a toaster. One interesting aspect of this work is that the authors of this paper cited at the bottom of the slide actually published in the paper a picture of their sticker so that anyone in the world could hypothetically download their paper, print out the sticker and stick it somewhere if they want to fool an AI system into thinking there's a toaster where there isn't. Now, I do not support anyone attacking AI systems to fool them into thinking there's toasters where they're none. But this shows unfortunately perhaps the ease with which one could hypothetically attack these AI systems.

### 防御手段存在但需要付出代价——修改神经网络可以让AI更难被攻击，但系统可能因此运行变慢；对于很多AI应用来说根本没人有动机去攻击（比如工厂里检测咖啡杯有没有划痕的系统），但对于垃圾邮件过滤、反欺诈等应用，攻击者会持续尝试突破防线，这就像一场"军备竞赛"——吴恩达分享了自己带领反欺诈团队时与欺诈者"全面开战"的经历，对方发动攻击后团队必须在几小时内做出反应来防御

> 那我们该怎么防御这些对抗性攻击呢？幸运的是，AI 界正在研发让它们更难被攻击的新技术。这些防御手段往往非常技术性，但有办法修改神经网络和其他 AI 系统来让它们更难被攻击。一个缺点是这些防御确实会带来一些代价——比如 AI 系统可能会运行得稍微慢一点。但这个领域的研究还在进行中，我们离拥有对所有重要应用都足够好的对抗性防御还很远。对很多 AI 系统来说，可能压根没人有动机去攻击。比如，你在工厂里运行一个自动视觉检测系统来检查咖啡杯有没有划痕——可能没什么人会有动机去骗你的系统让它以为一个有划痕的咖啡杯没有划痕。但一定会有一些 AI 应用场景，攻击是会出现的。对那些场景来说，我觉得——就像垃圾邮件与反垃圾邮件一样：垃圾邮件发送者想把垃圾邮件发进去，垃圾邮件过滤器在努力拦截它们——我认为将会出现这样的应用场景：我们 AI 社区一边在搭建防御，另一边有攻击者社区在尝试突破防御，双方进入"军备竞赛"。在我构建 AI 系统的经历中，少数让我感觉自己在跟别人"全面开战"的时刻，就是在带领反欺诈团队对抗欺诈者的时候。互联网上不幸存在一定量的欺诈行为——有人试图在支付系统里偷钱，或创建虚假账户。那几次做反欺诈系统的经历，是我少数真的感觉像在玩"零和游戏"的时刻：对手架起防御，他们立刻反应；他们发动攻击，我的团队有时必须在几个小时内做出反应来防御。所以我认为，在未来几年里，即使 AI 技术不断演进，也会存在一些像垃圾邮件、欺诈之类的垂直领域，团队会在对抗攻击者时感觉像在跟对手打"零和战争"。话虽这么说，我也不想过度夸大对抗性 AI 的潜在危害。对某些应用它确实极其重要，但也有很多 AI 应用场景不太可能遭受对抗性攻击。
> What can we do to defend against these adversarial attacks? Fortunately, the AI world has been working on new technologies to make them harder to attack. The defenses tend to be very technical, but there are ways of modifying new networks and other AI systems to make them somewhat harder to attack. One downside is that these defenses do incur some costs. For example, the AI system may run a little bit slower. But this is an area of ongoing research and we're far from having adversarial defenses that seem good enough for all the important applications that we want to apply AI to. For many AI systems, there may be no incentive for anyone to try to attack it. For example, if you're running a automatic visual inspection system to check if coffee mugs have scratches in your factory, maybe not that many people have any incentive to try to fool your system into thinking a scratch coffee mug doesn't have a scratch on it. But there are going to be AI applications where there will be attacks as well. For those applications, I think that similar to spam versus anti-spam, where spam is trying to get the spam email through and spam filters are trying to stop them. I think there will be applications where we will be in an arms race as the AI community is building up defenses and there is a community of attackers trying to fool our defenses. In my experience building AI systems, one of the few times I felt I was at total war with someone else was when I was leading anti-fraud teams fighting against fraudsters. There is unfortunately some amounts of fraud on the internet where people are trying to steal money and payment systems or create fraudulent accounts. The times I worked on anti-fraud systems was one of the few times that it really felt like a zero sum game had an adversary would put up a defense and they would react. They would launch an attack, my teams would have to react sometimes within hours to defend ourselves. So I think over the next few years, even as AI technologies evolve, there will be verticals like that, like spam, like fraud, where teams will be at war in what might feel like a zero sum game against adversaries. Having said that, I also don't want to overhype the potential of damage for adversarial AI systems. It is really important for some applications, but there are also plenty of AI applications which are less likely to be subject to adversarial attacks. Now in addition to adversarial attacks, AI unfortunately can also be used for some adverse or some negative use cases.
