### 自动驾驶中AI能做到的：输入一张车前方的照片（A）——可能来自摄像头、也可能结合雷达或激光雷达等传感器——输出其他车辆的位置（B）；今天的自动驾驶行业已经知道怎么收集足够的数据，也有相当不错的算法来做好这件事；但AI做不到的是：输入一段视频（A），理解一个人在对你的车做什么手势（B）——比如建筑工人举手示意你停车、搭便车的人在挥手拦车、骑自行车的人举左手表示要左转；问题在于人类的手势方式实在太多了，根本不可能收集到几千上万个人在各种不同方式下做手势的数据，而且理解手势意图本身就是一个复杂概念，连人类有时候都搞不清楚对方挥手到底想要什么

> 假设你在造一辆自动驾驶车。AI能做得很好的一件事是：拍一张你车前方的照片——可能只用摄像头，也可能结合雷达或激光雷达等其他传感器——然后判断其他车辆的位置、也就是其他车在哪里。这就是一个AI系统，输入A是你车前方的图片（或同时有图片以及雷达和其他传感器的读数），输出B是其他车辆的位置。今天自动驾驶行业已经搞明白了怎么收集足够的数据，也有相当好的算法来做好这件事。这就是今天AI能做到的。而今天AI做不到——或者至少用今天AI会非常困难——的事是：输入一张图片，输出人的意图或者这个人对你的车在做什么手势。比如这里有个建筑工人伸出手要你的车停下，这里有个搭便车的人在挥手拦车，这里有个骑自行车的人举左手表示要左转。如果你想构建一个系统来学习A到B的映射——输入A是一段人对你的车做手势的短视频，输出B是这个人的意图或想要什么——这在今天是极其困难的。部分问题在于：人向你做手势的方式多到数不清。想想看，一个人可能会用的所有手势——叫你慢下来、叫你走、叫你停——人们向你做手势的方式实在是太多太多了。所以你很难收集到足够的数据——几千甚至上万个人用各种不同方式对你做手势的数据——来捕捉人类手势的丰富性。所以从一段视频中学习这个人想要什么，其实是个相当复杂的概念。事实上，连人类自己有时候都很难判断一个向你的车挥手的人到底想要什么。其次，因为这是安全关键型应用，你会希望AI系统在判断建筑工人是想让你停还是想让你走上能极其准确——这也让AI系统更难做到。所以今天，如果你收集个一万张其他车辆的照片，很多团队都能构建一个至少具备基本其他车辆检测能力的AI系统。相比之下，即便你收集了一万个人的照片或视频——要找到一万个向你的车挥手的人就已经很难了——即便有这个数据集，我觉得今天也很难构建一个AI系统，能以安全驾驶所需的高精度来从手势中识别人的意图。这就是为什么今天很多自动驾驶团队有检测其他车辆的组件，也确实依靠这项技术来安全驾驶，但很少有自动驾驶团队会去尝试构建一个AI系统来识别大量多样化的人类手势，并仅靠这个来在人群中安全驾驶。

> Let's say you're building a self-driving car. Here's something that AI can do pretty well, which is to take a picture of what's in front of your car and maybe just using a camera, maybe using other sensors as well such as radar or LiDAR. And then to figure out what is the position or where are the other cars. So this would be an AI where the input A is a picture of what's in front of your car or maybe both a picture as well as radar and other sensor readings. And the output B is where are the other cars. And today the self-driving car industry has figured out how to collect enough data and has pretty good algorithms for doing this reasonably well. So that's what AI today can do. Here's an example of something that today's AI cannot do or at least would be very difficult using today's AI, which is to input a picture and output the intention or whatever the human is trying to gesture at your car. So here's a construction worker holding out a hand to ask your car to stop. Here's a hitchhiker trying to wave a car over. Here's a bicyclist raising the left hand to indicate that they want to turn left. And so if you were to try to build a system to learn an A to B mapping where the input A is a short video of a human gesturing at your car and the output B is what's the intention or what does this person want. That today is very difficult to do. Part of the problem is that the number of ways people gesture at you is very, very large. Imagine all the hand gestures someone could conceivably use, ask you to slow down or go or stop. The number of ways that people could gesture at you is just very, very large. And so it's difficult to collect enough data from enough thousands or tens of thousands of different people gesturing at you and all of these different ways to capture the richness of human gestures. So learning from a video to what this person wants is actually a somewhat complicated concept. In fact, even people have a hard time figuring out sometimes what someone waving at your car wants. And then second, because this is a safety critical application, you would want an AI system that is extremely accurate in terms of figuring out does the construction worker want you to stop or does he or she want you to go. And that makes it harder for an AI system as well. And so today, if you collect just say 10,000 pictures of other cars, many teams will build a built-in AI system that at least has a basic capability at detecting other cars. In contrast, even if you collect pictures or videos of 10,000 people, it's quite hard to track down 10,000 people waving at your car. Even with that data set, I think it's quite hard today to build an AI system to recognize human intention from the gestures at the very high level of accuracy needed in order to drive safely around these people. So that's why today, many self-driving car teams have some components for detecting other cars and they do rely on that technology to drive safely. But very few self-driving car teams are trying to counter an AI system to recognize a huge diversity of human gestures and counting just on that to drive safely around people.

### 医学诊断中AI能做到的：输入胸部X光片（A），输出有没有肺炎的诊断（B）——这件事AI能做到；但AI做不到的是：仅凭一本医学教科书里10张图片和几段文字来诊断肺炎——一个年轻的医学生可以看几十张图片再读几段文字就开始建立概念，但AI系统今天完全做不到这一点；因为你根本不知道给定一本教科书，该怎么定义A和B、怎么把这个任务变成一个AI问题

> 再看一个例子。假设你想构建一个AI系统来看X光图像并诊断肺炎。这些都是胸部X光片。输入A可以是X光图像，输出B可以是诊断——这个病人有没有肺炎？这件事AI能做到。AI做不到的事是：仅凭一本医学教科书里10张图片和几段关于肺炎的说明文字来诊断肺炎。人类可以看一小批图片——也许几十张——再读几段医学教科书的文字，就能开始有个大概的理解。但我真的不知道：给定一本医学教科书，什么是A什么是B？怎么把这个问题变成一个AI问题？如果你手上只有10张图片和几段解释肺炎在胸部X光片上是什么样子的文字，我真的不知道怎么写出一个软件来解决这个问题。而一个年轻的医学博士呢，读读医学教科书、看看几十张图片就能学得相当不错——相比之下，AI系统今天就是做不到这一点。

> Let's look at one more example. Say you want to build an AI system to look at x-ray images and diagnose pneumonia. So all of these are chest x-rays. So the input A could be the x-ray image and the output B can be the diagnosis. Does this patient have pneumonia or not? So that's something that AI can do. Something that AI cannot do would be to diagnose pneumonia from 10 images of a medical textbook chapter explaining pneumonia. A human can look at a small set of images, maybe just a few dozen images and read a few paragraphs from a medical textbook and start to get a sense. But I actually don't know, given a medical textbook, what is A and what is B or how to really pose this as an AI problem to let know how to write a piece of software to solve if all you have is just 10 images and a few paragraphs of text that explain what pneumonia and chest x-ray looks like. Whereas a young medical doctor might learn quite well reading a medical textbook and just looking at maybe dozens of images in contrast, an AI system isn't really able to do that today.

### 总结机器学习的强项和弱项：它擅长学习简单概念（你一秒钟内能判断的事）加上有大量数据的情况；它不擅长从少量数据中学习复杂概念；此外，AI还有一个被低估的弱点——当遇到训练数据之外的新类型数据时表现很差；比如用高质量医学中心的X光片训练出来的肺炎诊断AI，换到另一家医院——那里技师可能让病人斜着拍片，或者图像上有奇怪的划痕和异物——AI的表现就会大打折扣；相比之下，人类医生从左边那些高质量图像中学到的东西，更容易适应右边的图像——因为人能判断出"哦，病人只是躺歪了而已"，但AI系统的泛化能力远不如人类医生

> 总结一下机器学习的强项和弱项。机器学习往往在以下情况下效果很好：你学习的是一个简单概念——比如你一秒钟以内就能做出判断的事——并且有大量数据可用。机器学习往往在以下情况下效果不好：你试图从少量数据中学习一个复杂概念。AI另一个被低估的弱点是：当遇到跟训练数据中见过的数据类型不一样的新数据时，AI的表现往往很差。让我用一个例子来解释。假设你构建了一个监督学习系统，用A到B来学习从这些图片中诊断肺炎——这些是高质量的胸部X光片。但现在假设你把这个AI系统拿去另一家医院或另一个医学中心使用——那里拍X光的技师不知怎么搞的，总让病人斜着躺着拍，或者图像上有这些缺陷（不知道你能不能看到图片上那些小划痕、这些落在病人身上的小东西）。如果AI系统是从左边那些数据中学出来的——可能来自一个高质量医学中心——然后你把这个AI系统拿到另一个产生右边那种图像的医学中心去用，它的表现也会很差。一个好的AI团队能够改善或减少这些问题，但这并不容易做到。这也是AI比人类弱很多的地方之一——如果一个人从左侧的图像中学到了东西，他们更有可能适应右侧的图像，他们能判断出"病人只是躺歪了"。但AI系统在泛化或者说"搞清楚怎么处理新类型数据"方面，可能远不如人类医生。

> To summarize, here are some of the strengths and weaknesses of machine learning. Machine learning tends to work well when you're trying to learn a simple concept such as something that you could do with less than a second of mental thought and when there's lots of data available. Machine learning tends to work poorly when you're trying to learn a complex concept from small amounts of data. A second underappreciated weakness of AI is that it tends to do poorly when it's also performed on new types of data that's different than the data it has seen in your dataset. Let me explain with an example. Say you built a supervised learning system that uses A to B to learn to diagnose pneumonia from images like these. These are pretty high quality chest x-ray images. But now let's say you take this AI system and apply it at a different hospital or a different medical center where maybe the x-ray technician somehow strangely had the patients always lie at an angle or sometimes there are these defects. Not sure if you can see the little scratches in the image. These little other objects lying on top of the patients. If the AI system has learned from data like that on your left, maybe taken from a high quality medical center and you take this AI system and apply it to a different medical center that generates images like those on the right, then this performance will be quite poor as well. A good AI team would be able to ameliorate or to reduce some of these problems but doing this is not that easy. And this is one of the things that AI is actually much weaker than humans. If a human has learned from images on the left, they're much more likely to be able to adapt to images like those on the right as they figure out that the patient is just lying at an angle. But an AI system can be much less robust than human doctors in generalizing or figuring out what to do with new types of data like this.
