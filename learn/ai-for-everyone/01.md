### AI热潮背后有一个重要的概念区分——你今天看到的大量AI价值来自"弱人工智能"（ANI），也就是只会做一件事的"一招鲜"，比如智能音箱、自动驾驶、网页搜索；而ChatGPT这类生成式AI的出现让AI变得更通用了一些；至于能做任何人类智力任务的"通用人工智能"（AGI），虽然研究在一步步往前挪，但我们离它还有几十年甚至上百年——别被那些"邪恶机器人统治人类"的炒作吓到了

> 人们对 AI 有很多兴奋，但也有很多不必要的炒作。原因之一是 AI 其实是两个不同的概念。我们今天看到的大部分 AI 价值来自"弱人工智能"——Artificial Narrow Intelligence，简称 ANI。这些 AI 只会做一件事，比如智能音箱、自动驾驶汽车、网页搜索用的 AI，或者农业和工厂里的 AI 应用。这类 AI 是"一招鲜"——但当你找到了合适的那一招，它就能创造巨大的价值。而随着生成式 AI 的崛起，像 ChatGPT 和 Bard 这类工具的出现，AI 开始变得更通用了一些。比如 ChatGPT 可以做文字编辑、头脑风暴伙伴、文本摘要，还能帮你完成很多其他任务。这些模型是一个令人兴奋的进展，进一步拓展了我们用 AI 能做的事情。此外，AI 还指代另一个概念——AGI，也就是通用人工智能。这是要造出能做人类能做的任何智力任务的 AI，甚至可能是超级智能，比任何人类能做的事都多。我看到弱人工智能和生成式 AI 在飞速进步，AI 研究也在一小步一小步地朝 AGI 靠近，这很让人激动。但现实是，我们离 AGI 还非常非常远。不幸的是，弱人工智能和生成式 AI 的快速进步——它们确实非常有价值——让人们得出"AI 在飞速发展"的结论，这倒没错。但人们又进一步错误地认为，我们可能快要实现 AGI 了，这就导致了一些过度膨胀的、不必要的恐惧，比如"有意识的邪恶机器人要来统治人类"之类的。我认为 AGI 是研究者的一个激动人心的目标，但在到达那里之前还需要很多次技术突破。可能要几十年，甚至上百年——希望如此，但我不确定我们这辈子能不能看到。既然 AGI 还那么遥远，我觉得没必要为它过分焦虑。
> There's a lot of excitement, but also a lot of unnecessary hype about AI. One of the reasons for this is because AI is actually two separate ideas. A large amount of the value we see from AI today is artificial narrow intelligence or ANI. These are AIs that do one thing such as a smart speaker or a self-driving car or AI for a web search or AI applications in farming or in a factory. These types of AI are one trick ponies. But when you find the appropriate trick, this can be incredibly valuable. With the rise of generative AI, things like ChatGPT and Bard, we're also starting to see AI does a bit more general purpose. For example, ChatGPT can be a copy editor, brainstorming partner, text summarizer, and help with many other tasks. These models have been an exciting development and this further expanding what we can now do with AI. In addition, AI also refers to the concept of AGI or artificial general intelligence. This is the goal of building AI that could do any intellectual tasks that a human can or maybe even be super intelligent and do even more things than any human can. I'm seeing tons of progress in ANI, artificial narrow intelligence, as well as on generative AI. And it feels like AI research is slowly taking baby steps, tiny baby steps, toward AGI, which is exciting. But realistically, we're still very far from AGI or artificial general intelligence. Unfortunately, the rapid progress in ANI and generative AI, which are incredibly valuable, that has caused people to conclude that there's a lot of progress in AI, which is actually true. But that in turn has caused people to falsely think that we might be on the verge of AGI as well, which is leading to some overblown and unnecessary fears about evil sentient robots coming to take over humanity. I think AGI is an exciting goal for researchers to work on, but it'll take multiple technological breakthroughs before we get there. And it may be decades, maybe even hundreds of years, I hope, but I'm not sure if we will get there in our lifetimes. But given how far away AGI is, I think there is no need to undue the anxiety about it.
